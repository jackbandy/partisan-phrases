Mr. KAINE. Mr. President, pursuant to section 601(b) of the  International Security Assistance and Arms Export Control Act, I move  to discharge the Committee on Foreign Relations from further  consideration of S.J. Res. 104, to direct the removal of United States  Armed Forces from hostilities within or against the Islamic Republic of  Iran that have not been authorized by Congress.
