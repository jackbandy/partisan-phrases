No New Wars


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700426/no-new-wars


Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to oppose President Trump's  illegal, unconstitutional, and reckless war in Iran.

We are 5 days in, and we have lost six brave American servicemembers,  and thousands of Americans are stranded abroad. My prayers are with  their families and with everyone who is in harm's way.

After yesterday's briefing, I am more convinced than ever that there  was no imminent threat to America, and there is no clear endgame  strategy.

Americans need affordable healthcare, groceries, and lower gas  prices, not a war that costs $220 million a day and puts our troops in  danger without a clearly defined mission.

That is why I will vote for the bipartisan War Powers Resolution to  rein in this out-of-control President and end his unauthorized  hostilities in Iran.
