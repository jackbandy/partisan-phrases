Curb President Trump's Use of Military Force in Iran


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700540/curb-president-trumps-use-of-military-force-in-iran


Ms. ROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with grave concerns about  President Trump's unwarranted and unconstitutional war of choice on  Iran.

Only Congress has the authority to declare war. The President does  not have the power to send our Nation to war unilaterally.

We are now in an escalating conflict that the American people do not  want and that Congress did not authorize.

Senior Trump administration officials have failed to provide  significant evidence of an imminent threat to justify this attack, and  there is no consensus on when it will end.

What is their plan to prevent further loss of American  servicemembers? What will stop our country from being pulled into  another protracted conflict in the Middle East? How much will it cost?

This is why I just voted for a War Powers Resolution to curb  President Trump's use of military force in Iran and protect American  lives.
