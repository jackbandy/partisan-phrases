Recognizing the Investiture of Judge William Mercer


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700551/recognizing-the-investiture-of-judge-william-mercer


Mr. DOWNING. Mr. Speaker, I rise today as Montanans gather for the  investiture ceremony of Judge William Mercer, marking an important  moment for our state and judicial system.

Since 1994, Bill Mercer has served as the assistant United States  attorney for the District of Montana, the acting United States  Associate Attorney General, and as a member of the Montana House of  Representatives in addition to a stint as a practicing attorney and  partner at law firm Holland and Hart in Billings.

Throughout his judicial endeavors, Bill developed a reputation as a  respected and principled practitioner of the law. On July 10, 2025, he  was nominated to serve as United States district judge of the United  States District Court for the District of Montana by President Donald  J. Trump--a distinct honor in and of itself.

Bill brings a wealth of experience to the bench, and I have complete  confidence that he will uphold the rule of law and serve the people of  Montana with integrity, professionalism, and honor. I congratulate Bill  and best of luck as he embarks on this new chapter.
