Subcommittee Memberships of the Committee on Appropriations for the 119th Congress


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701583/subcommittee-memberships-of-the-committee-on-appropriations-for-the-119th-congress


The new assignments reflect Senator Deb Fischer's selection as chair  of the Subcommittee on Legislative Branch and Senator Jon Husted's  selection as a member of the Subcommittee on Agriculture, Rural  Development, Food and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies;  Subcommittee on Financial Services and General Government; Subcommittee  on Department of the Interior, Environment, and Related Agencies;  Subcommittee on Departments of Labor, Health and Human Services, and  Education, and Related Agencies; Subcommittee on Legislative Branch;  and Subcommittee on State, Foreign Operations, and Related Programs,  all for the remainder of this Congress.
