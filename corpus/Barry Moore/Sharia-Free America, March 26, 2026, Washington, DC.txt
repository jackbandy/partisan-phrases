Sharia-Free America


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701398/sharia-free-america


Mr. MOORE of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I thank the gentleman from Texas  for yielding.

Mr. Speaker, America has one law, the United States Constitution. It  is the foundation of our Republic and the ultimate authority in our  legal system. It applies equally to every person in every place and in  every case.

The principle of equal justice under the law is what sets our Nation  apart and ensures that no one is above the law and no one is beneath  it.

Sharia law is not simply a matter of personal religious belief or  private practice, it is a comprehensive legal and political framework.

Where it has been enforced around the world, it has often led to  restrictions on basic freedoms that Americans recognize not only as God  given, but constitutionally protected.

In practice, sharia law has been used to target and disadvantage  women, limit the rights of religious minorities, and punish political  dissent.

Our system is built on individual liberty, accountability, and  fairness, not on unequal treatment or restrictions based on belief,  gender, or background.

No foreign legal code should ever influence American courts and  create a parallel system of justice. Allowing outside legal framework  to seep into our system would undermine the consistency and integrity  of American law.

Sharia law must never be allowed to override the Constitution in any  kind of way. America does not bend itself to radical ideologies,  whether they come from within or outside our borders.

Our duty is to preserve and defend the freedoms that generations of  Americans have fought to protect. That means remaining vigilant,  upholding our Constitution, and ensuring that liberty, equality, and  justice remain the guiding principles of the United States of America.
