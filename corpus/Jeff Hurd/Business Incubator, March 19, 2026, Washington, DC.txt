Mr. HURD of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 40th  anniversary of the Business Incubator Center and its extraordinary  impact on Mesa County and Colorado's western slope.

For four decades, the Business Incubator Center has been a driving  force behind economic growth: supporting entrepreneurs, strengthening  small businesses, and creating opportunities in communities across the  region. Its work reflects the very best of local innovation and  leadership.

The impact is clear. The Incubator has supported 33 percent of all  businesses in Mesa County and has helped create more than 14,000 jobs.  These are not just numbers, they are families supported, livelihoods  sustained, and stronger communities.

Through mentorship, resources, and a commitment to helping others  succeed, the Business Incubator Center has empowered generations of  businessowners to build something meaningful and lasting in western  Colorado.

I congratulate the Business Incubator Center on 40 years of success  and in recognizing its lasting impact on the people and communities it  serves.
