Moment of Silence to Honor the Life and Service of Corporal Timothy O'connor, Jr.


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700834/moment-of-silence-to-honor-the-life-and-service-of-corporal-timothy-oconnor-jr


Ms. HOULAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today, joined by my colleagues from  the Pennsylvania delegation, to honor the life and the service of  Corporal Timothy O'Connor, Jr., a Pennsylvania State trooper and a  proud member of our Chester County community.

Corporal O'Connor wore the uniform of the Pennsylvania State Police  for 15 years, serving the people of our Commonwealth. He was part of  the brotherhood and sisterhood of troopers who run toward danger so  that others can live in safety.

Two weekends ago, while conducting a traffic stop in the line of  duty, Corporal O'Connor was tragically shot and killed.

Today we stand here together to honor his courage, his sacrifice, and  his service. We hold his wife, Casey, their young daughter, and the  entire Pennsylvania State Police family in our hearts.

Mr. Speaker, please do join us for a moment of silence to honor the  life of Corporal Timothy O'Connor.
