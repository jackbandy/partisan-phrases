Recognizing Steely Lumber Company


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1702097/recognizing-steely-lumber-company


Mr. LUTTRELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Steely Lumber  Company, a local Texas sawmill, which is celebrating their 130th  anniversary next month.

Across 6,000 acres in Walker County, Kelvin Steely and his family  didn't just build a business. They built a legacy. Every inch of it is  rooted in hard work, producing 100 percent southern yellow pine used  for decking, housing, and construction.

With the help of their 125 amazing employees, Steely Lumber now makes  six times more lumber than they did in the 1990s.

Steely Lumber also prides itself on creating long-term benefit from  the land for profitability, wildlife, and conservation.

These efforts have allowed them to grow not only in lumber production  but also in related businesses that reduce waste.

Mr. Speaker, for 130 years, Steely Lumber Company has made our  community proud. Through generations of grit, integrity, and hard work,  they have set the standard for what it means to build something that  lasts.

On behalf of the Eighth Congressional District, I congratulate them  on their legacy and success, and I thank them for all they have done  for our fellow Texans.
