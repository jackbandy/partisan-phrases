Recognizing Noah Bontrager As the First Hoosier High School Student to Run A Mile in Under 4 Minutes


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700963/recognizing-noah-bontrager-as-the-first-hoosier-high-school-student-to-run-a-mile-in-under-4-minutes


Mr. STUTZMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  extraordinary achievement of Westview senior Noah Bontrager for  becoming the first high school student to run a sub 4-minute mile in  Indiana history.

This past Sunday, Noah competed at the New Balance Nationals race  where the country's highest performing high school athletes compete in  track events.

Hailing from Shipshewana, Indiana, Noah made Indiana's Third  Congressional District proud as he clinched first place in the 1-mile  race.

Not only did Noah set a new record for Hoosiers, but also made his  mark by claiming the seventh fastest indoor mile time for high school  students in U.S. history.

Noah has demonstrated incredible talent, and his achievements  indicate his level of dedication to his sport. He has done what no  other Hoosier student has been able to do.

Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Noah on this huge accomplishment. I know  he will continue to set new records as he continues his running career  at Notre Dame University this fall.
