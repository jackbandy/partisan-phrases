California's 2026 Distinguished Schools


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701830/californias-2026-distinguished-schools


Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate nine schools in  my district that have been honored as California's 2026 Distinguished  Schools.

Congratulations to the students, teachers, and staff at California  School of the Arts-San Gabriel Valley in Duarte; Edgewood High School  in West Covina; El Monte High School in El Monte; Hollencrest Middle  School in West Covina; Lone Hill Middle School in San Dimas; Mt. SAC  Early College Academy at West Covina; Ramona Middle School in La Verne;  Sandburg Middle School in Glendora; and South Hills High School in West  Covina.

The California Distinguished Schools recognition program honors  public schools that go above and beyond to close education gaps,  provide exemplary instruction, and innovate education systems to help  create better outcomes for our students.

Our students are our future, and I am so proud to see these inspiring  schools receive the recognition they deserve.

The students, teachers, and staff at these distinguished schools are  to be commended for their dedication and hard work. I congratulate them  on this tremendous achievement.
