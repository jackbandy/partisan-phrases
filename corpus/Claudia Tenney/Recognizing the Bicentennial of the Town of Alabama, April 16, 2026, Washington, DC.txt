Recognizing the Bicentennial of the Town of Alabama


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701820/recognizing-the-bicentennial-of-the-town-of-alabama


Ms. TENNEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the  Bicentennial of the Town of Alabama, located in Genesee County, New  York. In the early 19th century, settlers began to gather in the  region, appreciating the abundant resources the land offered. By 1828,  the previously named town of Gerrysville was officially renamed  Alabama. Within the town there are four hamlets: Alabama Center, Basom,  South Alabama, and Wheatville.

For 200 years, Alabama has respected and maintained its strong  farming heritage. The fertile land has proven Alabama and the  surrounding area as one of the foremost agricultural regions within the  State. Agricultural growers and producers continue to responsibly  steward the land, providing fresh products to our region and country.

As the town celebrates its 200th anniversary, I join my colleagues in  wishing its residents a wonderful year of celebration and many more  future milestones.
