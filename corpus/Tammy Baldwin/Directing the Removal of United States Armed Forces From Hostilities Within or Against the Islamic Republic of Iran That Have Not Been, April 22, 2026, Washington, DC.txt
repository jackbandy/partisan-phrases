Ms. BALDWIN. Pursuant to section 601(b) of the International Security  Assistance and Arms Export Control Act, I move to discharge the  Committee on Foreign Relations from further consideration of S.J. Res.  114 to direct removal of U.S. Armed Forces from hostilities within or  against the Islamic Republic of Iran that have not been authorized by  Congress.

Ms. BALDWIN. I know of no further debate.
