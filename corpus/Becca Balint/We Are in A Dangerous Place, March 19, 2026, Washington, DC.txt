We Are in A Dangerous Place


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700948/we-are-in-a-dangerous-place


Ms. BALINT. Mr. Speaker, Americans do not want to live in a country  where masked agents can carry you away in an unmarked car.

Americans don't want to live in a country where innocent people are  just dumped on the side of the road in freezing temperatures because  ICE agents made a mistake and arrested the wrong person.

They don't want to live in a country where agents can bust down the  doors to their homes without a warrant.

They don't want to be stopped while they are out shopping and have  somebody in a mask and armed to the teeth asking them where their  papers are.

None of these things happen in a free country. They don't.

We are in a dangerous place right now.

We have a choice, all of us. We can either speak out and defend the  rights that are given to us in our Constitution, or we can watch them  slip away.

Those are the stakes. We have to push back. We have to speak out and  use our voices against this tyranny.
