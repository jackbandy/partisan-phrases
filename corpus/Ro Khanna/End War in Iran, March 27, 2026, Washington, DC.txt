End War in Iran


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701492/end-war-in-iran


Mr. KHANNA. Mr. Speaker, this body needs to vote on   Thomas Massie's  and my resolution to end the war in Iran.

The President has sent 2,000 more soldiers from the Army's 82nd  Airborne Division to Iran. This is on top of the 4,500 marines we  already have there.

We cannot afford to put ground troops in Iran. This body needs to say  no to the $200 billion request and vote to end a war that is causing  gas to skyrocket. Gas is up to nearly $4 a gallon, up 34 percent, and  we have already lost 13 American servicemembers.

Let's end the war. Let's bring the troops home. Let's make sure we  are putting Americans first.
