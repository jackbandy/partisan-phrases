Mr. MURPHY. Mr. President, pursuant to section 601(b) of the  International Security Assistance and Arms Export Control Act, I move  to discharge the Committee on Foreign Relations from further  consideration of S.J. Res. 116, to direct the removal of the United  States Armed Forces from hostilities within or against the Islamic  Republic of Iran that have not been authorized by Congress.

Mr. MURPHY. Mr. President, I know of no further debate.

Mr. MURPHY. I would ask for the yeas and nays.
