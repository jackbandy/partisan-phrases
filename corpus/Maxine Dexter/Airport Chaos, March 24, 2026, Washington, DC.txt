Airport Chaos


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701197/airport-chaos


Ms. DEXTER. Mr. Speaker, people just want their time in airports to  be uneventful. They want to get through security, get to their gate,  get to their home and their families, or onto their travels without  chaos, delays, or confusion.

The White House is making that impossible. Rather than working with  Democrats to fund TSA, Trump is sending ICE agents into our country's  airports. ICE agents are not trained in aviation security or customer  service. They are not able to help TSA agents, who have been working  without pay for 47 percent of this fiscal year. ICE agents create fear,  confusion, and certainly more chaos in our airports.

There is a better way. Republicans should focus on sending paychecks,  not ICE, to our airports, and let Americans get where they are going  safely and uneventfully.

Stay loud.
