Making Our Skies Safer


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701673/making-our-skies-safer


Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise in strong support of the  bipartisan ALERT Act.

Last year, 67 lives were lost in a tragic midair collision of a  flight from my home State of Kansas to Washington, D.C.

Those 67 people were parents, children, and friends. Their families'  lives changed in an instant. In the weeks that followed, I met with  those families. I listened and made a commitment to turn their grief  into action.

This bipartisan bill reflects that work. It takes commonsense steps  to make our skies safer, modernizing collision avoidance technology,  strengthening training, and closing dangerous communications gaps.

It also acts on the National Transportation Safety Board  recommendations, turning lessons learned into real action.

We cannot bring those 67 people back, but we can honor them by  preventing this from happening ever again.

I urge my colleagues on both sides of the aisle to vote ``yes'' and,  after the passage of this bill, work together on a final, fully  thought-out bill that ensures safety measures are efficiently  implemented. The families deserve nothing less.
