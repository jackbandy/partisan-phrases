Recognizing the Service of Liliana Callahan


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701570/recognizing-the-service-of-liliana-callahan


Ms. SCHAKOWSKY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  contributions of Liliana Callahan, Staff Assistant and Constituent  Advocate in my district office, and to thank her for the dedication and  hard work she brought to serving residents throughout the 9th  Congressional District of Illinois.

Liliana grew up in Maryland and came to Chicago to attend Loyola  University Chicago, eventually deciding to put down roots here. Soon  after graduating in 2023, she joined our team full time. Though she was  the newest and youngest member of our staff, she quickly established  herself as someone we could count on.

During her early time with our office, Liliana managed front-line  constituent contact and oversaw all United States Postal Service  casework across the 9th District. She also directed our district office  intern program for several semesters, ensuring each group of interns  had the resources and guidance they needed to succeed.

Over time, Liliana developed a specialization in immigration  casework. She built a deep skill set and was an excellent advocate on  behalf of constituents. Many people who reached out to our office  feared for the safety and long-term security of their family members  navigating our country's immigration system, and Liliana's compassion  and expertise reassured them that we were on their side and ready to  help.

Liliana worked extremely hard to help and advocate for constituents,  while managing a large workload. She made a difference in the lives of  so many constituents. We heard from many of them who were grateful for  her work, including the constituent who wrote to me: ``I wanted to  express my heartfelt gratitude for your instrumental role in securing  my father's I-485 approval. Your dedication and expertise made all the  difference, and we are overjoyed to share the news of his successful  adjustment of status. Thank you for your outstanding work. We  appreciate your dedication to making a difference in our lives and look  forward to your continued leadership.''

On behalf of the residents of the 9th Congressional District of  Illinois, I thank Liliana Callahan for her service. She did not grow up  here, but she showed up, did the work, and made a real difference for  the people of this district. I am proud of her accomplishments and  confident she will continue to build on what she started here.
