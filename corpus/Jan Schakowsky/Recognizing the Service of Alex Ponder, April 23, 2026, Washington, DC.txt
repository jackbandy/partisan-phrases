Recognizing the Service of Alex Ponder


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1702175/recognizing-the-service-of-alex-ponder


Ms. SCHAKOWSKY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Alex Ponder  for her many contributions and 7 years of service to 9th Congressional  District residents as Senior Advisor for District Operations and  Outreach on my district staff. Alex has spent the formative years of  her career serving the community where she grew up.

Alex was born and raised in the district in Park Ridge and interned  with my office while studying at Loyola University Chicago. She made  such a strong impression that we invited her to join our team full time  after graduation. Over the years, she grew into a trusted and capable  member of my staff, serving her community with care and consistency.

In her role, Alex was a constant presence across the district. She  built lasting relationships with residents and community organizations  and helped ensure our office stayed connected to the people we serve.  She skillfully led our Congressional Art Competition, working closely  with educators and students to build a program that was well run and  drew strong participation from schools across the district each year,  lifting up young artists and recognizing their work.

Alex was someone my staff knew they could always count on. She  stepped into scheduling, communications, and other roles whenever  needed, never treating any responsibility as outside her lane. She  helped keep track of casework numbers and distribution and was an  essential part of our office team during many transitions. Alex began  as an intern in our district office and later came to oversee that same  program. She selected and managed the interns who assist our office  each semester, helping bring new members onto ``Team Schakowsky'' and  teaching them how to advocate for residents and support a congressional  office.

Alex knows how to make constituents feel welcome and listened to. She  is kind and compassionate, and helped create a warm environment for  constituents who called or visited the office.

Alex advocated for constituents in a number of important areas, and  handled a high volume of constituent casework. She became the passport  expert in our office and was able to cut through red tape to get  constituents their passports quickly, alleviating their anxiety during  what is often a stressful process. She helped seniors with Social  Security and Medicare, and helped constituents resolve problems with  the IRS and immigration. Alex made a real, concrete difference in the  lives of so many 9th Congressional district residents.

Alex received a well-deserved and special recognition that reads  ``Agudath Israel of Illinois hereby recognizes the extraordinary  efforts of Alex Ponder. Her dedication to the constituents of Illinois'  9th Congressional District, under the leadership of Congresswoman Jan  Schakowsky, is truly remarkable. As a global pandemic affected new  challenges for countless individuals, you gave personal attention to  every person in need. Regardless of the hour or day of the week, and  often against the odds, you worked tirelessly to ensure needs were met,  emergencies were addressed, and best outcomes achieved. On behalf of  Agudath Israel and our entire community, we express profound gratitude  for constantly going above and beyond the call of duty.''

And a constituent wrote this heartfelt note ``Your employee Alex  Ponder of the Glenview office has gone way way above and beyond in  assisting our daughter and her family this summer . . . I reached out  to Alex Ponder and she spent as much time as necessary to guide us  through the various documents and notarizations and other forms  necessary. She also arranged a meeting with the U.S. Passport Office in  Chicago and followed through to be sure all was accomplished  efficiently and properly. This is government service at its best, Jan,  and I smile every day as I drive past your house, happy to have you as  a neighbor as well as our representative! Young professionals such as  Alex are worth their weight in gold, and it's a testament to your  office that such talent is recruited and retained.''

On behalf of the residents of the 9th Congressional District of  Illinois, I want to express  my sincere gratitude to Alex Ponder for her years of service. Her work  on our team and her commitment to her community reflect her character  and values. I am proud of how much she has accomplished here and know  that she will continue to succeed and serve the community in the years  ahead.
