Recognizing Ms. Cathy Tatum


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701461/recognizing-ms-cathy-tatum


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my  outstanding constituents, Ms. Cathy Tatum, a dedicated volunteer whose  steadfast commitment, compassion, and hard work have made a profound  difference through Seniors in Service AmeriCorps Seniors RSVP Pasco.  Since joining in 2024, Cathy has become one of those dependable  volunteers who shows up consistently, jumps in wherever needed, and  ensures essential services continue for neighbors facing hardship, She  began volunteering through Seniors in Service at CARES, and when that  program closed, she immediately transitioned to supporting food  assistance. Today, she serves at the St. Vincent de Paul Food Pantry in  Port Richey at Our Lady Queen of Peace, a vital but small pantry  supporting an extremely vulnerable population, including individuals  living paycheck-to-paycheck or experiencing homelessness.

Cathy brings patience, compassion, and a steady heart to every task.  She packs food boxes, assists in the office, and helps with bookkeeping  and client files--whatever the pantry needs that day, she does it with  dedication and care. Her commitment is extraordinary: in November 2025,  she logged 69 hours of service, and in December 2025, she contributed  an incredible 173 hours in a single month. This level of dedication  speaks volumes about her work ethic and the depth of her care for her  community. Equally important is the way Cathy gives her time. blown for  her warmth, kindness, and ability to make others feel welcome, she  consistently supports her team and the clients served with positivity,  respect, and dignity. Her steady presence ensures that the pantry can  reach more neighbors in need, and that every person who walks through  the door feels valued.

For her extraordinary dedication, compassionate service, and  meaningful impact on the community, I am proud to recognize Ms. Cathy  Tatum as a 2026 Hero Among Us. I ask my colleagues to join me in  honoring her unwavering commitment and the lasting difference she  continues to make for the people of Pasco County.
