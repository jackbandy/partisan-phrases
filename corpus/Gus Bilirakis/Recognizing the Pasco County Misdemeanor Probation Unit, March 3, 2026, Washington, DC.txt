Recognizing the Pasco County Misdemeanor Probation Unit


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700366/recognizing-the-pasco-county-misdemeanor-probation-unit


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the members of  the Pasco County Misdemeanor Probation Unit--Tracy Toner, Edward  Shadick, Michele Rinaldi, Marissa Parker, Venus Long, Sarah Demedeiro,  I'Shorn McClendon, Jaclyn Knapp, and Danai Jerrido--for their  extraordinary service and collective impact on our community. Together,  they exemplify what it means to make a profound and lasting difference  in the lives of others through compassion, integrity, and unwavering  dedication. This exceptional team plays a vital role in guiding  individuals toward accountability and positive change. Their work  extends far beyond fulfilling professional responsibilities. Each day,  they provide direction, structure, and access to critical resources  that empower individuals to meet their obligations and build more  stable futures. They approach every case and every interaction with  patience, professionalism, and a genuine belief in the power of  personal growth.

What sets this group apart is their collaborative spirit and  commitment to strengthening the broader community network. They work  closely with community partners, service providers, and stakeholders to  ensure that individuals have access to employment opportunities,  treatment programs, and supportive services while maintaining stability  in their lives. This coordinated and thoughtful approach not only  supports individuals on their journey but reinforces the health and  safety of our entire community. Their work is guided by the highest  ethical standards. They consistently demonstrate integrity,  accountability, and fairness, ensuring that responsibilities are met  while treating every individual with dignity and respect. Through  careful case management and consistent follow-through, they help reduce  recidivism, promote long-term behavioral change, and foster safer  neighborhoods. Equally important is their recognition that justice and  compassion can work hand in hand. Their efforts contribute to restoring  stability for those impacted by wrongdoing and reinforce the principle  that accountability, paired with opportunity, can lead to meaningful  transformation.

Every day, this team makes a measurable difference in the lives of  individuals and families throughout our community. Their dedication  reflects a shared belief that sometimes one supportive voice, one  structured plan, or one person willing to invest the time can alter the  trajectory of a life. For their professionalism, compassion, and  steadfast commitment to service, I proudly recognize these outstanding  individuals as 2026 Heroes Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional  District. Their work embodies the very spirit of community leadership  and demonstrates that positive change is possible when accountability  is paired with support and opportunity.
