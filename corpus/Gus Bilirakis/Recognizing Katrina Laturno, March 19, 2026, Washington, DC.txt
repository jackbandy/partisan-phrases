Recognizing Katrina Laturno


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700957/recognizing-katrina-laturno


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the  extraordinary contributions of one of my constituents, Katrina Laturno,  a dedicated volunteer with the Citrus County Fair Association. Ms.  Laturno serves on the Board of Directors of the Fair and is an active  member of the Livestock Committee. She is deeply engaged in the  community on behalf of the Fair and has demonstrated exceptional  commitment to its success. Through her initiative and leadership, she  has written and presented grants to various organizations, helping the  Fair secure additional funding to promote, enhance, and expand its  programs. Ms. Laturno has gone above and beyond in her service to the  Fair and the Fair Board, generously giving her time, expertise, and  persistence to see these grants through from concept to completion. Her  efforts have strengthened the Fair and ensured it remains a vibrant and  meaningful tradition for our community. It is for these reasons that we  are proud to nominate her for this award. Mr. Speaker, part of what  makes our nation so great is the willingness of ordinary Americans to  selflessly give back to their communities. Volunteerism is a  cornerstone of our society and a vital thread in the fabric that unites  us. Ms. Laturno embodies this spirit of service in every sense.

It is clear that Katrina Laturno has made a profoundly positive  impact on our community, and I am deeply grateful for her dedication  and service. I am proud to honor her accomplishments by recognizing her  as a 2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
