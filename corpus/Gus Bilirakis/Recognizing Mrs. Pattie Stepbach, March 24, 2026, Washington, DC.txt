Recognizing Mrs. Pattie Stepbach


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701171/recognizing-mrs-pattie-stepbach


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  extraordinary contributions of a dedicated community leader in my  district, Mrs. Pattie Stepbach. Mrs. Stepbach has been a beacon of  compassion, generosity, and unwavering commitment to the well-being of  students in our community.

Through her extraordinary work with Operation Heartfelt, Mrs.  Stepbach has directly and profoundly impacted the lives of students by  ensuring they have access to essential food supplies each weekend. Each  week, she coordinates, prepares, and delivers food bags for the Weekend  Backpack Program, a vital initiative that helps bridge the nutritional  gap for students facing food insecurity. Her consistent presence in  this program brings stability, dignity, and hope to countless students  who rely on this support to meet their basic needs. Mrs. Stepbach's  dedication goes far beyond routine service. She leads efforts to gather  donations, organize deliveries, foster partnerships, inspire  volunteerism, and advocate for youth who might otherwise go unseen.  Thanks to her leadership and Operation Heartfelt's commitment, Central  High School students who once faced uncertain weekends now have  reliable nourishment and the opportunity to focus on their academic and  personal growth. Mrs. Stepbach embodies the qualities of a true hero:  selfless service, tireless dedication, and a profound impact on the  lives of others. Her efforts demonstrate that one person's kindness can  create a ripple effect that strengthens an entire community.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mrs.  Pattie Stepbach for her outstanding contributions to our community. For  her compassion, leadership, and unwavering commitment to the students  of Hernando County, I am proud to honor Mrs. Stepbach as a 2026 Heroes  Among Us recipient for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
