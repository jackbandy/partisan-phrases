Recognizing Mr. Howie Taylor


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700614/recognizing-mr-howie-taylor


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding  community leader, mentor, and advocate, Mr. Howie Taylor, whose  dedication to strengthening families and empowering youth makes him an  exceptional candidate for the 2026 Bilirakis Heroes Among Us  Congressional Award. A longtime volunteer with Big Brothers Big Sisters  of Tampa Bay, Howie has devoted years to transforming the lives of  young people through consistent mentorship, heartfelt connection, and  unwavering advocacy. His mentoring journey began with his first Little,  Hunter--a relationship so impactful that Howie was named Big Brother of  the Year during their match. Today, he continues that legacy of service  with his second Little, Elijah, carrying forward the same commitment to  guidance,  endouragement, and accountability. In both mentoring relationships,  Howie has intentionally emphasized the values of gratitude,  responsibility, and service. One of his most meaningful teaching tools  is shared volunteerism. By taking his Littles alongside him to serve  the community, he instills the importance of giving back and  appreciating opportunities. Through these experiences, he helps shape  young men into respectful, productive, and compassionate leaders of  tomorrow.

Beyond his one-on-one mentorship, Howie serves as Chair of the Big  Brothers Big Sisters Community Leadership Council in Pasco County. In  this role, he plays a pivotal part in building partnerships, raising  awareness, and expanding opportunities for youth who are waiting to be  matched with mentors. His leadership ensures that more children have  access to positive and consistent role models during some of the most  formative years of their lives. Howie's impact is amplified by his  vibrant personality and diverse interests. A passionate volunteer and  avid car enthusiast, he has even entertained fans as a pirate aboard  the Tampa Bay Buccaneers ship. In every setting, whether professional,  civic, or recreational, Howie uses his platform to elevate the mission  of Big Brothers Big Sisters of Tampa Bay and inspire others to get  involved.

He is also a tireless advocate for the Florida Big Brothers Big  Sisters specialty license plate initiative, actively raising financial  support and encouraging adults to step forward as mentors. His advocacy  is fueled by a sincere belief that every child deserves guidance,  stability, and hope. Professionally, Howie extends this same spirit of  service through his work with Coast Dental, leveraging his role to  support young people and families in need and creating pathways of care  that extend far beyond his office walls.

Through mentorship, leadership, advocacy, and professional service,  Howie Taylor exemplifies what it means to be a hero among us. His  unwavering commitment to empowering youth and strengthening his  community makes him not only a remarkable volunteer, but a true role  model worthy of recognition. For his extraordinary dedication and  lasting impact, I am proud to honor Howie Taylor today as a 2026 Hero  Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
