Recognizing Brittney Forcino


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700328/recognizing-brittney-forcino


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding  community leader, Brittney Forcino, whose professional excellence and  heartfelt commitment to service have made a meaningful impact  throughout Brooksville and the surrounding region. In the fast-paced  world of professional marketing and property management, it is rare to  find someone who successfUlly balances high-level corporate  responsibility with genuine, hands-on community involvement. As  Marketing Director for Lowman Law Firm, Brittney expertly manages the  marketing strategy and rental portfolios for a large tri-county  practice. Yet, it is her work beyond the office walls that truly sets  her apart. Brooksville Main Street is proud to work alongside Brittney,  who has become a creative force behind many of our community events.  She possesses a unique ability to transform ordinary event spaces into  unforgettable experiences--``amazing escapes''--that captivate families  and bring people together. Her creativity is never simply decorative;  it is intentional. Each idea is thoughtfully designed to foster family  fun, encourage community engagement, and create welcoming environments  for residents of all ages.

Through her leadership, Brittney demonstrates that corporate  sponsorship can be personal, interactive, and heart-led. She seamlessly  reflects the core values of Lowman Law Firm--community and caring--by  ensuring that the firm's presence at local events is meaningful and  memorable.

Brittney leads with focus, warmth, and authenticity. Her team at the  firm holds her in high regard, and that admiration extends well beyond  her workplace. She approaches every initiative with a ``community- first'' mindset, ensuring that Brooksville's events are inclusive,  engaging, and reflective of the spirit that defines our town. Her  service does not stop there. Brittney is an active volunteer with  Jayne's Dance Academy and a proud, dedicated sports mom who can often  be found on the sidelines cheering on the next generation. Her ability  to balance professional success, volunteerism, and family life--all  while inspiring those around her--speaks volumes about her character.  Brittney Forcino does not merely attend events; she pours her heart  into them. Her enthusiasm and dedication have elevated the quality of  our community gatherings and strengthened the bond between local  businesses and the families they serve. She exemplifies what it means  to be a true community leader--someone who understands that success is  not measured solely in professional accomplishments, but in the lives  uplifted along the way.

Brooksville is stronger, more vibrant, and more connected because of  Brittney's tireless work and creative spirit. I am grateful for her  service and proud to recognize her as a 2026 Hero Among Us for  Florida's 12th Congressional District.
