Recognizing Pastor Tom Reusch


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700602/recognizing-pastor-tom-reusch


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Pastor Tom  Reusch and The Chapel Church for their extraordinary and ongoing impact  on students, families, and educators throughout Pasco County Schools.  Under Pastor Tom's steadfast leadership, The Chapel Church has become  far more than a house of worship--it has become a trusted and  indispensable community partner.

Guided by generosity, compassion, and humility, Pastor Tom has  cultivated a culture of service that meets both the tangible and  emotional needs of our school communities. One of the most meaningful  examples of this commitment is The Chapel's annual Thanksgiving  outreach. Each year, families across multiple schools who sign up  receive a complete Thanksgiving meal, ensuring they can gather around  the table with dignity, joy, and gratitude. This effort does more than  provide food--it strengthens community bonds and reminds families that  they are seen, valued, and supported. During the Christmas season, The  Chapel extends that same spirit of generosity to students participating  in programs such as Pack-A-Sack, purchasing and delivering numerous  Christmas gifts. These thoughtful acts ease financial burdens for  families facing economic hardship and create moments of joy and  excitement for children who might otherwise go without. For many  students, these gestures send a powerful message: their community cares  deeply about them. The Chapel's service does not end with seasonal  outreach. Under Pastor Tom's leadership, volunteers regularly roll up  their sleeves to improve school campuses--building new student desks,  assisting with campus enhancement projects, and contributing hands-on  labor to create welcoming, functional learning environments. Their work  helps ensure that students have spaces that inspire confidence and  opportunity. Equally impactful is their support for educators and  staff. At schools such as Fox Hollow Elementary, The Chapel has  provided refrigerators, microwaves, and stocked staff lounges with  snacks and refreshments. While these may appear to be simple acts, they  carry profound meaning. They acknowledge the tireless dedication of  teachers and staff and reinforce that their hard work does not go  unnoticed. What truly sets Pastor Tom and The Chapel Church apart is  their consistency and holistic approach to service.

They respond not only to physical needs, but also to relational  ones--showing up at school events, listening with empathy, and building  genuine partnerships that strengthen the connection between schools and  the broader community. In every respect, Pastor Tom Reusch exemplifies  servant leadership. Through faith in action, he has helped create a  bridge of hope, unity, and encouragement for countless families. The  Chapel Church's contributions have strengthened Pasco County Schools  and inspired others to serve alongside them.

For their unwavering commitment and extraordinary generosity, I am  proud to recognize Pastor Tom Reusch and The Chapel Church as a 2026  Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District. Their work is  helping build brighter futures for our students and a stronger  foundation for our community.
