Recognizing Ellen Frank


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701531/recognizing-ellen-frank


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an  extraordinary member of our community, Ellen Frank, whose compassion,  resilience, and unwavering dedication to serving others make her a  truly deserving recipient of the 2026 Heroes Among Us Congressional  Award. Ellen Frank exemplifies the very best of servant leadership  through her remarkable volunteer work with People Helping People (PHP)  in Hernando County, Florida. As an active and hands-on volunteer, Ellen  prepares meals, delivers food and essential supplies to individuals  experiencing homelessness, and ensures that our most vulnerable  neighbors are treated with dignity and care. What makes Ellen's service  even more extraordinary is that she has continued her tireless  volunteer efforts while undergoing cancer treatment, including  chemotherapy. Even in the face of serious health challenges, Ellen has  chosen to focus not on her own hardships, but on uplifting others. Her  strength of spirit and steadfast commitment are nothing short of  inspiring. In addition to her direct service in the field, Ellen serves  on the Board of Directors for People Helping People. She plays a vital  role in fundraising efforts, sorts donated items, packs care bags for  those in need, and even drives the organization's vehicles to homeless  outreach sites when necessary. She does whatever is required--quietly,  faithfully, and without seeking recognition.

Through it all, Ellen remains upbeat and cheerful, offering  encouragement to fellow volunteers and hope to those she serves. Her  positive attitude in the midst of personal adversity is a testament to  her character and her deep-rooted belief in helping others. Ellen  Frank's selflessness, perseverance, and compassion have made a profound  impact on Hernando County. She embodies the true meaning of community  service and reminds us that even in life's most difficult seasons, we  can still be a light for others. For her extraordinary dedication, her  resilience in the face of adversity, and her unwavering commitment to  serving those in need, I am honored to recognize her as a 2026 Hero  Among Us. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Ellen Frank for  her outstanding service and example to us all.
