Recognizing Christine Smith


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701584/recognizing-christine-smith


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the  extraordinary contributions of one of my constituents, Christine Smith,  who has devoted nearly five years of service to Habitat for Humanity of  the Nature Coast. Over that time, Chris has volunteered more than 1,200  hours--an incredible commitment that has helped countless Habitat  partner families receive the keys to their new homes. Many of our  regular customers fondly know her as the ``Book Lady,'' as she  continues to volunteer in the media department at the Inverness  ReStore, carefully organizing books by author and genre. Chris  approaches every task with passion and dedication, often staying late  or arriving early to ensure the work gets done.

Even in retirement from a long career in school administration, Chris  continues to serve her community, supporting her peers and sharing her  talents. She is also an active member of a local chorus, bringing joy  to others through her music. Chris is not only a committed volunteer  but also a cherished member of the Habitat family.

It is clear that Christine Smith has made a profoundly positive  impact on our community. I am deeply grateful for her dedication and  service, and I am proud to recognize her as a 2026 Hero Among Us for  Florida's 12th Congressional District.
