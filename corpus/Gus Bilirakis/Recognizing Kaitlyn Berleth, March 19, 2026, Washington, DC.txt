Recognizing Kaitlyn Berleth


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701011/recognizing-kaitlyn-berleth


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor one  of my constituents, Kaitlyn Berleth, Therapist and Lead Clinician at  You Thrive Children's Advocacy Center, for her exceptional service to  some of the most vulnerable members of our community. Ms. Berleth has  made a profound and lasting impact on the lives of child abuse victims  through compassionate, skilled, and trauma-informed therapeutic care.  In her role, she provides ongoing trauma therapy while also delivering  critical crisis intervention services--offering immediate emotional  support and stabilization to children and families during some of the  most difficult moments they will ever face, Her calm presence, clinical  expertise, and unwavering professionalism create a safe and steady  environment in times of acute distress.

Kaitlyn approaches each child with patience, empathy, and clinical  excellence, meeting them where they are emotionally and  developmentally. By utilizing evidence-based trauma therapy techniques,  she helps children build healthy coping skills, emotional regulation,  and resilience--tools that positively shape their lives far beyond the  therapy room. In addition to serving children, Ms. Berleth provides  therapeutic support to adult survivors of sexual abuse. She fosters an  empowering and respectful space where individuals can process trauma,  reclaim their sense of agency, and move toward long-term healing. She  also offers vital guidance and reassurance to caregivers, helping  families navigate the complex journey of recovery. Families  consistently express gratitude for her compassion, professionalism, and  steadfast commitment.

Mr. Speaker, the strength of our Nation is reflected in professionals  who dedicate their lives to serving others with integrity and  selflessness. Kaitlyn Berleth embodies the mission of You Thrive  Children's Advocacy Center and the highest ideals of community service.  It is clear that her work has made an extraordinary and deeply positive  difference in countless lives. For these reasons, I am proud to  recognize Kaitlyn Berleth as a 2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th  Congressional District and commend her for her outstanding service.
