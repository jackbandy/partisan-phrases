Recognizing Carl and Carla Haberland


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701002/recognizing-carl-and-carla-haberland


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize two  extraordinary members of our community, Carl and Carla Haberland, whose  unwavering commitment to volunteerism has left a lasting imprint on  Brooksville Main Street and the families we serve. In every thriving  downtown, there are individuals who quietly and consistently step  forward to do the work that makes celebration and community possible.  Carl and Carla Haberland are those individuals for Brooksville. Their  dedication goes far beyond attendance at events they invest their time,  energy, and heart into ensuring our community traditions not only  continue, but flourish. What truly sets Carl and Carla apart is their  spirit of service, especially evident during the holiday season. Each  year, as Brooksville transforms into a winter wonderland, their  fingerprints are on every detail. From helping assemble the towering  40-foot Christmas tree to assisting with the installation of large  rooftop decorations, they embrace the physically demanding work that  brings magic to our downtown. These efforts are no small undertaking.  They require long hours, heavy lifting, climbing heights, and  meticulous coordination. Yet Carl and Carla approach every task with  enthusiasm and purpose, working side-by-side with fellow volunteers  simply because they care deeply about the joy these decorations bring  to families throughout our community.

Their service is not motivated by recognition or applause. It is  driven by love for their town and a desire to create cherished memories  for children and adults alike. Because of their dedication, generations  of families have experienced the wonder of a beautifully decorated  downtown during the most meaningful time of the year. Carl and Carla  Haberland exemplify what it means to be true community builders. They  remind us that the strength of a town lies not only in its buildings  and businesses, but in the generosity and teamwork of the people who  call it home. Brooksville is brighter--quite literally--because of  their efforts.

It is my privilege to honor Carl and Carla Haberland for their  extraordinary volunteerism and their steadfast commitment to bringing  joy and unity to our community and recognize them as 2026 Heroes Among  Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
