Recognizing Ms. Miss Coyle


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701139/recognizing-ms-miss-coyle


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  extraordinary contributions of a dedicated community leader in my  district, Ms. Missy Coyle. Missy has devoted the past 14 years to  coalition and volunteer work, using her time, talent, and expertise to  strengthen the Pasco County community.

As the current Coordinator for the Alliance for Substance Addiction  Prevention, Missy oversees the largest prevention organization in the  county. She coordinates community events, leads initiatives to support  those in need, and consistently shows up day in and day out at the All  Ways Center to provide assistance, guidance, and compassion to  individuals facing challenges.

Missy's dedication extends to times of crisis. During the 2024  hurricane season, she played a critical role in transforming the All  Ways Center into a hurricane respite center, helping hundreds of  residents access essential resources and support when they needed it  most. A veteran and a nurse, Missy brings her skills, discipline, and  empathy to every aspect of her service, ensuring that her community  receives care, stability, and hope. Her tireless commitment,  leadership, and selflessness make a profound difference in the lives of  those she serves. Missy Coyle exemplifies the spirit of ``Heroes Among  Us,'' giving of herself to improve her community and uplift those in  need.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms. Missy  Coyle for her outstanding dedication and service. For her compassion,  leadership, and unwavering commitment to the people of Pasco County, I  am proud to honor Missy Coyle as a 2026 Heroes Among Us recipient for  Florida's 12th Congressional District.
