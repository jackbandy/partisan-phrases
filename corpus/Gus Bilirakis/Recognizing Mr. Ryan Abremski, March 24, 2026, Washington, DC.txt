Recognizing Mr. Ryan Abremski


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701152/recognizing-mr-ryan-abremski


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  extraordinary contributions of a dedicated community leader in my  district, Mr. Ryan Abremski. Ryan's outstanding leadership, generosity,  and unwavering commitment to improving the lives of seniors in Pasco  County are quite remarkable. As President of the Pasco Aging Network  (PAN) for the past two years, Ryan has devoted himself to strengthening  the organization's impact, expanding its visibility, and enhancing  services for the elder population in our community.

Founded in 1989, PAN is a coalition of public and private  organizations committed to serving seniors, providing a vital forum for  professionals to network, share ideas, and disseminate information  about elder services. Under Ryan's leadership, PAN has experienced  renewed energy, stronger engagement, and increased community awareness.  He has worked tirelessly to ensure that monthly meetings are not only  opportunities for networking, but also meaningful educational  experiences that equip providers with the tools and knowledge necessary  to better serve older adults. Ryan understands that collaboration is  the cornerstone of effective community service. He has strengthened  partnerships among healthcare providers, social service agencies,  nonprofit organizations, and local businesses, ensuring that seniors in  Pasco County benefit from a more connected and responsive support  system.

His leadership has reinforced PAN's mission to expand information- sharing opportunities, promote education, and develop special projects  that enhance services for the elder population. One of Ryan's most  remarkable contributions has been his ability to translate vision into  tangible results. Through the company he works for, he secured a  $60,000 donation for PAN, an extraordinary achievement that  significantly expanded the organization's capacity to fulfill its  mission. Beyond financial support, Ryan has worked diligently to  increase community awareness about the needs of older adults and the  resources available to them. He has been a visible and passionate  advocate, consistently promoting PAN's activities and encouraging  greater involvement from both public and private stakeholders. Ryan's  leadership is characterized by integrity, collaboration, and genuine  compassion. He leads not for recognition, but because he believes  deeply in serving others--especially those who have contributed so much  to our community throughout their lives. His work ensures that seniors  in Pasco County have access to coordinated services, informed  providers, and a network that is continuously striving to meet their  evolving needs.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Ryan  Abremski for his dedication, vision, and transformative impact on  the Pasco Aging Network and the seniors it serves. For his tireless  commitment to improving the lives of older adults in our community, I  am proud to honor Ryan Abremski as a 2026 Heroes Among Us recipient for  Florida's 12th Congressional District.
