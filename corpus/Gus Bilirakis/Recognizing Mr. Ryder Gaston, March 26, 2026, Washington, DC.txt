Recognizing Mr. Ryder Gaston


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701343/recognizing-mr-ryder-gaston


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr, Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of our  outstanding community members, Mr. Ryder Gaston, whose tireless  dedication has brought vital holistic health care services to veterans,  military personnel, first responders, and law enforcement through  Cryoeeze 22.

Mr. Gaston and his family--his wife, Mrs. Tami Gaston, his sons  Remington and River Gaston, and his daughter-in-law Chealsey Gaston-- have provided thousands of free services aimed at supporting physical  recovery, mental health, and overall wellness. Their work focuses on  addressing traumatic brain injuries, reducing veteran suicide rates,  and assisting with an array of other physical and mental health  challenges. Through their efforts, countless individuals in our  community have received healing, hope, and care they might not  otherwise have had access to. Ryder Gaston exemplifies selflessness,  determination, and a profound commitment to helping others. His  facility, Cryoeeze 22, provides a safe and supportive environment where  veterans and other community members can pursue recovery and wellness  without cost or barrier. He is constantly seeking new ways to serve  those in need, demonstrating the highest ideals of compassion, service,  and leadership.

For his unwavering dedication, his selfless service, and the profound  impact he has made on the lives of veterans, first responders, and our  community at large, I am proud to recognize Mr. Ryder Gaston as a 2026  Hero Among Us. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his  extraordinary commitment and the lasting difference he continues to  make.
