Recognizing Mr. Willard ``will'' Hoffman


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701465/recognizing-mr-willard-will-hoffman


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my  outstanding constituents, Mr. Willard ``Will'' Hoffman, a dedicated  volunteer whose skill, energy, and generosity keep the Free Senior  Health Clinic running smoothly and bring joy to countless members of  our community.

Will is the clinic's ``Mr. Fix-It,'' tackling every repair and  maintenance need--from fixing faucets and toilets, to cleaning floors  and bathrooms, painting, and organizing--always with reliability and  good cheer. He also meticulously manages all storage areas, ensuring  the clinic operates efficiently and effectively. Beyond his hands-on  work, Will dedicates his Mondays to calling bingo for Star One  participants with disabilities, delighting them with prizes and  bringing smiles to their faces week after week.

For his unwavering dedication, practical skill, and meaningful impact  on the lives of patients and volunteers alike, I am proud to recognize  Mr. Hoffman as a 2026 Hero Among Us. I ask my colleagues to join me in  honoring his selfless service, tireless work ethic, and the lasting  difference he makes every day at the clinic and in our community.
