Recognizing Mr. Jay Gundy


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700612/recognizing-mr-jay-gundy


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an exceptional  volunteer whose quiet dedication has made a profound and lasting impact  on the Disability Achievement Center--Mr. Jay Gundy. Since beginning  his volunteer service in May 2024, Jay has exemplified the very best of  community commitment. Month after month, he dedicated approximately ten  hours of his time to one of the most essential--yet often unseen-- functions of the organization: inventorying donated durable medical  equipment. While this work may occur behind the scenes, it is critical  to ensuring that individuals with disabilities receive the  equipment they need in a timely and efficient manner. Through  meticulous attention to detail and thoughtful analysis, Jay quickly  recognized opportunities to strengthen the agency's inventory systems.  He did not simply complete assigned tasks--he sought ways to improve  them. His recommendations led to transformative, agency-wide  improvements in how donated equipment is organized, tracked, and  accessed. Today, because of Jay's efforts, the Disability Achievement  Center operates with a far more streamlined and efficient warehouse  system, ensuring that vital equipment is readily available to those who  depend upon it.

His contributions produced measurable impact. By significantly  reducing the time staff and management previously devoted to inventory  management, Jay helped free countless hours that can now be redirected  toward serving individuals with disabilities and expanding the  organization's reach within the community. Yet Jay's legacy extends  beyond systems and logistics. He brought with him a positive attitude,  steady professionalism, and genuine kindness that uplifted staff and  consumers alike. He became not just a volunteer, but a trusted and  valued member of the team. His humility and heart for service were  evident in every interaction. When unforeseen circumstances required  Jay to step away from volunteering, his absence was deeply felt. In a  heartfelt letter, he expressed gratitude for the opportunity to serve  and for the warm welcome he received--a testament to his character and  the sincerity with which he approached his work.

Jay Gundy's dedication, compassion, and meaningful contributions  embody the true spirit of a Hero Among Us. His service reminds us that  lasting change is often driven by individuals willing to invest their  time, talents, and heart in helping others.

It is my honor to recognize Jay Gundy for his extraordinary  volunteerism, the enduring difference he has made in the lives of so  many, and as a 2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional  District.
