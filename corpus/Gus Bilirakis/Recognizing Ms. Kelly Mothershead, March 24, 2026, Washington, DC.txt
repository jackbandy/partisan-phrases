Recognizing Ms. Kelly Mothershead


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701249/recognizing-ms-kelly-mothershead


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the exceptional  contributions of a dedicated community leader in my district, Ms. Kelly  Mothershead. Kelly is an extraordinary advocate for the Pasco County  community, demonstrating a tireless commitment to improving the lives  of her neighbors and strengthening local organizations.

For many years, Kelly has served on the Board of the Alliance for  Healthy Communities, consistently showing up at events, assisting with  fundraising, and lending her expertise and energy to support the  organization's mission. Her dedication goes beyond traditional board  responsibilities; she has created a scholarship fund to provide  transitional living opportunities for those in need, ensuring  vulnerable members of our community have a pathway to stability and  independence.

Everything Kelly does, she does with heart, soul, and unwavering  compassion. Her efforts have a lasting impact on the community,  fostering hope, opportunity, and stronger support systems for Pasco  County residents. Kelly Mothershead exemplifies the spirit of service  and selflessness that defines a true community hero.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms. Kelly  Mothershead for her outstanding dedication to the people of Pasco  County. For her generosity, leadership, and tireless advocacy, I am  proud to honor Kelly Mothershead as a 2026 Heroes Among Us recipient  for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
