Recognizing Paul Friedlander


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700601/recognizing-paul-friedlander


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an  extraordinary servant leader whose impact on the Rotary Club of Trinity  and the greater Pasco County community has been immeasurable--Paul  Friedlander, Paul has served the Rotary Club of Trinity with  distinction, including as President and in nearly every leadership  capacity within the organization. His commitment to service, however,  extends well beyond the club itself. Through his involvement with  Rotary District 6950, he has supported more than 50 regional clubs,  strengthening the broader Rotary mission of ``Service Above Self'' and  fostering collaboration across our region.

Paul truly embodies the spirit of servant leadership. When a homeless  family needed emergency shelter and essential provisions, Paul was  there. When fire victims required immediate clothing and assistance, he  mobilized his trusted ``hotline'' of generous donors without  hesitation. Time and again, he is the first to respond, the first to  organize, and the first to ensure that no one in our community faces  hardship alone. His leadership reflects a rare and powerful combination  of institutional knowledge and heartfelt compassion. Paul understands  not only how to effectively organize service efforts, but why they  matter. He recognizes that true service is rooted in empathy,  responsiveness, and a steadfast commitment to lifting others up. Beyond  Rotary, Paul's record of civic engagement is equally noteworthy. He has  served multiple terms as President of the Community Service Council,  sits on the BayCare Hospital Board, and holds the distinction of being  our community's longest-reigning King Chasco--a testament to the  respect and admiration he has earned throughout the region.

As the current President of the Rotary Club of Trinity has shared,  Paul's wisdom and experience continue to guide the organization's work.  His mentorship has cultivated new leaders, strengthened community  partnerships, and ensured that Rotary's mission remains vibrant and  impactful in Pasco County. Words alone cannot fully capture the depth  of Paul's contributions. His legacy is not written in accolades, but in  action--in the families assisted, the lives uplifted, and the stronger,  more compassionate community that exists because he chose to serve. For  his unwavering dedication, compassionate leadership, and extraordinary  commitment to the people of Pasco County, I am proud to recognize Paul  Friedlander as a 2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional  District. He exemplifies the very best of community service and the  enduring power of servant leadership.
