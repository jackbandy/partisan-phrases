Recognizing Lynn Skelton


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700590/recognizing-lynn-skelton


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Lynn Skelton, a  dedicated community leader whose longstanding commitment to service has  made a meaningful and enduring impact throughout Pasco County. Having  relocated to Florida at the age of two and proudly calling Pasco County  home since 1984, Ms. Skelton has demonstrated a deep and sustained  investment in the growth, stability, and well-being of her community.  Her heart for  service is perhaps most evident through her extensive involvement with  Habitat for Humanity, where she has generously shared her time, talent,  and resources for many years.

Through her company, ProSource of Port Richey, Lynn has donated  flooring materials and sponsored multiple Habitat projects, directly  supporting families striving to achieve the dream of homeownership. Her  contributions have done more than improve physical structures--they  have helped transform houses into homes, providing families with  stability, dignity, and renewed opportunity.

Lynn's leadership extends well beyond material generosity. She served  on the Habitat for Humanity Board of Directors from 2021 to 2022 and  later on the Executive Committee from 2023 to 2024, helping shape the  organization's strategic direction and strengthen its mission. Most  recently, she continued her civic engagement through service on the  Pasco Leadership Council from 2024 to 2025, further demonstrating her  dedication to responsible leadership and community advancement. Lynn  attributes her commitment to service to a profound sense of gratitude-- gratitude for her faith, for her community, and for the personal growth  she has experienced throughout her life. That gratitude has  consistently translated into meaningful action, as she seeks  opportunities to give back and uplift those around her.

Her example reminds us that strong communities are built not only by  institutions, but by individuals willing to lead with humility,  generosity, and vision. For her steadfast dedication, generous spirit,  and lasting contributions to Pasco County, I am proud to recognize Lynn  Skelton as a 2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional  District.
