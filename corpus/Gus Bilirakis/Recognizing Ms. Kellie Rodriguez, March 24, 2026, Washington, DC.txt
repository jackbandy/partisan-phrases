Recognizing Ms. Kellie Rodriguez


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701257/recognizing-ms-kellie-rodriguez


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  extraordinary contributions of a dedicated community leader in my  district, Ms. Kellie Rodriguez. Kellie is a community builder,  prevention leader, and recovery advocate whose work across Pasco County  exemplifies the spirit of the Congressional ``Heroes Among Us'' Award.

As Executive Director and CEO of the Alliance for Healthy Communities  and President of The Hope Shot, Kellie has devoted her life to  strengthening individuals, families, and neighborhoods through  prevention, recovery, and community connection. Her leadership is  rooted in both professional expertise and lived experience. As someone  in long-term recovery herself, she leads with empathy, transparency,  and an unwavering belief that people and communities can heal and  thrive. Under Kellie's guidance, the Alliance for Healthy Communities  has grown into a multi-coalition organization addressing substance use  prevention, youth leadership, mental wellness, and suicide prevention  across Pasco County. She has united diverse sectors of the community-- including schools, law enforcement, healthcare, businesses, faith  organizations, and local government--to develop data-driven,  compassionate, and sustainable solutions.

One of Kellie's most significant accomplishments is the co-creation  of the All Ways Center in New Port Richey with The Hope Shot. This  innovative facility is the county's first Prevention and Recovery  Resource Center, housing prevention and recovery services under one  roof. The center serves as a hub for education, support, and connection  for residents throughout the region, ensuring that individuals and  families have access to critical resources and guidance. Kellie is also  deeply engaged in civic life. She is an active Rotarian with Holiday  Rotary, a mentor and collaborator with local hospitals, schools,  behavioral health providers, and government leaders. Her work has  expanded youth prevention initiatives, promoted recovery-friendly  community efforts, and strengthened partnerships that enhance Pasco  County's overall health and resilience. Known as a connector,  visionary, and compassionate leader, she brings people together across  sectors to create meaningful change.

Through tireless service, innovative leadership, and a steadfast  commitment to others, Kellie Rodriguez continues to transform lives and  strengthen the fabric of her community. Her work ensures that residents  across Pasco County have access to prevention, recovery, and the  support they need to thrive.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms. Kellie  Rodriguez for her extraordinary dedication and impact. For her  compassion, vision, and transformative service, I am proud to honor  Kellie Rodriguez as a 2026 Heroes Among Us recipient for Florida's 12th  Congressional District.
