Recognizing Mr. Mike Tutko


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701460/recognizing-mr-mike-tutko


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my  outstanding constituents, Mr. Mike Tutko, a dedicated volunteer whose  selfless service and leadership have made a profound impact on Hernando  County through his work with the United Way and the Society of St.  Vincent de Paul.

Mike began his journey with the United Way of Hernando County 11  years ago as a Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) preparer, earning  certification through the IRS program. Through dedication, consistency,  and natural leadership, he quickly became Site Coordinator, overseeing  tax preparation operations at the Brooksville location and mentoring  fellow volunteers each season. Under his guidance, the Brooksville site  alone has served more than 2,460 low-to-moderate income families,  helping them save an estimated $984,000 in tax preparation fees--funds  that families instead used for rent, medical care, utilities, and food.  Mike's expertise, careful quality review, and deep knowledge of tax law  make the Brooksville VITA site a trusted and reliable resource. He and  his team go far beyond filing returns, assisting clients with identity  theft issues, navigating Earned Income Tax Credit eligibility,  understanding direct deposit options, and resolving issues that could  delay refunds. His patience, advocacy, and thoughtful guidance ensure  that every individual receives the support they need during what can  often be a stressful process.

Beyond the VITA program, Mike has a long-standing commitment to  service through the Society of St. Vincent de Paul, where he assists  families facing hardship. Through home visits, compassionate listening,  and careful follow-up, he identifies meaningful solutions and provides  emergency assistance and financial support. Most recently, Mike played  a critical role in supporting a family through a Hurricane Milton Long- Term Recovery case, helping a single mother and her two children secure  a safe, stable home, so they could begin rebuilding their lives. A  proud military veteran, Mike brings the values of service, integrity,  and selflessness to every role he takes on. His leadership, dedication,  and compassion consistently go above and beyond, touching countless  lives throughout Hernando County.

For his unwavering commitment, profound impact, and tireless service,  I am proud to recognize Mr. Mike Tutko as a 2026 Hero Among Us. I ask  my colleagues to join me in honoring his exceptional service, steadfast  advocacy, and the lasting difference he has made in our community.
