Recognizing Brandon Mowatt


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700339/recognizing-brandon-mowatt


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an  extraordinary member of our community, Brandon Mowatt, a dedicated  volunteer with Brooksville Main Street whose tireless efforts have  helped fuel the continued revitalization of our historic downtown. When  anyone thinks of the engine that keeps Brooksville Main Street running,  they think of Brandon. Whether representing his business, 3 Brothers  Detailing, or serving simply as a committed citizen in work boots and  ready to lend a hand, Brandon is the definition of a powerhouse  volunteer. The success of large-scale community events depends on the  unseen work--the grit and sweat poured in before the first guest  arrives and long after the last one leaves. Brandon is at the very  heart of that effort. He is consistently the first to arrive for setup  and the last to leave after breakdown. From coordinating the logistical  complexities of vendor load-in to tackling the less glamorous but  essential tasks like cleanup and trash removal, Brandon approaches  every responsibility with an enthusiasm that is truly contagious.

What sets Brandon apart is not only his willingness to work, but his  ability to lead and innovate. He is a strategic thinker who is always  several steps ahead. He does not simply move a tent--he considers how  its placement can improve flow, enhance vendor visibility, and create a  better experience for attendees, He holds himself and those around him  to a high standard, ensuring that every Brooksville Main Street event  is executed with excellence. His energy transforms long, physically  demanding days into meaningful team-building experiences for fellow  volunteers. Perhaps the most powerful testament to Brandon's character  occurred earlier this year. In January 2026, Brandon and his family  endured a devastating personal loss. in the midst of such profound  grief, no one would have expected him to show up--in fact, many would  have urged him to take time away. Yet his dedication to Brooksville and  its community runs so deep that he chose to volunteer during the  Tangerine Festival. In one of the most difficult seasons of his life,  he still showed up for others, offering strength and reliability when  it would have been entirely understandable to step back.

Brandon Mowatt does not simply volunteer; he invests his heart and  soul into the betterment of Brooksville. His impact is measured not  only in the countless hours he gives, but in the seamless execution of  community events and the uplifted spirits of those who serve alongside  him. He is a rare find, a selfless leader, and truly deserving of this  recognition. Brandon Mowatt embodies the spirit of a Hero Among Us.  Through his generosity, quiet leadership, and unwavering commitment to  service, he has made a meaningful and lasting difference in our  community.

It is clear that Mr. Brandon Mowatt has made an extraordinary and  deeply positive impact on countless lives. I am profoundly grateful for  his dedication and service and proud to recognize him as a 2026 Hero  Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
