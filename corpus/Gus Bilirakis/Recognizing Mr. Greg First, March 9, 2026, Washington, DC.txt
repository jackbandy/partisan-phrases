Recognizing Mr. Greg First


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700596/recognizing-mr-greg-first


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an  extraordinary servant leader and veteran, Mr. Greg First, whose  selfless dedication to his community embodies the very spirit of the  2026 Heroes Among Us Congressional Award. As a proud United States Air  Force veteran, Greg has carried his commitment to service far beyond  his time in uniform. For the past five years, he has faithfully  volunteered at the Metropolitan Ministries East Pasco Family Support  Center, where he has become an indispensable member of the team, During  that time, he has completed more than 250 volunteer shifts and  contributed over 860 hours of hands-on, compassionate service to  individuals and families in need. Greg's work is often quiet but deeply  impactful. Whether answering phones, comforting a caller in crisis,  assisting families seeking essential support, or guiding guests through  available resources, he approaches every responsibility with patience,  empathy, and genuine care. His steady presence offers reassurance  during some of life's most difficult moments.

What sets Greg apart is not only his consistency, but the warmth he  brings to every interaction. Staff, volunteers, and clients alike  describe him as a source of hope and encouragement. His positive energy  and contagious enthusiasm create an atmosphere of dignity and welcome  throughout the East Pasco campus. Simply put, he lifts spirits simply  by being present. Beyond his daily volunteer efforts, Greg's deep roots  in the East Pasco community strengthen the mission of Metropolitan  Ministries. Through longstanding local relationships and authentic  community engagement, he helps build meaningful connections that expand  outreach and ensure that support reaches those who need it most.

Greg's faithful and humble service reflects the very best of our  Nation's values. He does not seek recognition or applause. Instead, he  quietly and consistently works to improve the lives of others-- motivated not by reward, but by conviction and compassion. In every  sense, Greg First exemplifies what it means to be a hero among us. His  dedication to both country and community serves as an inspiring  reminder that the call to serve does not end with military service--it  continues in the everyday acts of kindness that strengthen our  neighborhoods and uplift our fellow citizens. For his extraordinary  commitment, uplifting presence, and steadfast devotion to serving  others, I am proud to recognize Greg First as a 2026 Hero Among Us for  Florida's 12th Congressional District.
