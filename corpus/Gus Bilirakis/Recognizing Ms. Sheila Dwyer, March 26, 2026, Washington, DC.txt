Recognizing Ms. Sheila Dwyer


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701440/recognizing-ms-sheila-dwyer


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my  outstanding constituents, Ms. Sheila Dwyer, a dedicated volunteer whose  tireless leadership and commitment have made a profound difference  through Seniors in Service AmeriCorps Seniors RSVP Pasco. Sheila serves  as a site coordinator for the VITA tax preparation program, ensuring  that seniors and low-income residents in Pasco County have access to  free, reliable, and trustworthy tax support--a service many could not  afford or access elsewhere. This season alone, Sheila has completed  nearly 1,000 tax returns, serving individuals who rely on this program  to stay financially stable. Senior citizens on fixed incomes and  working individuals living paycheck to paycheck regularly tell her,  ``They don't know what they would do without this program.'' Sheila has  earned their trust through her professionalism, consistency, and  genuine care. Her commitment extends far beyond preparing returns.  Sheila manages scheduling, client coordination, and team  communications, ensuring that a high-volume site runs smoothly. At one  point this season, she sent an email at 1:07 a.m. reporting that the  program had already reached over 1,706 appointments, predicting they  would exceed 2,000. Her tireless dedication--even working around the  clock to meet the needs of her clients--reflects both her remarkable  work ethic and her deep belief in the importance of this service.

Even when the demand became so intense that Sheila literally lost her  voice from preparing returns and making calls, she ensured the mission  continued by coordinating with her team and asking for help,  guaranteeing that seniors and other clients continued to receive the  support they needed. Sheila exemplifies the best of community service:  tireless, practical, and deeply committed to helping others.

For her extraordinary dedication, steadfast leadership, and  meaningful impact on the lives of countless residents, I am proud to  recognize Ms. Sheila Dwyer as a 2026 Hero Among Us. I ask my colleagues  to join me in honoring her exceptional service and the lasting  difference she has made in Pasco County.
