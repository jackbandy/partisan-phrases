Recognizing Ms. Halleen


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700361/recognizing-ms-halleen


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding  contributions of an exceptional community leader in my district, Ms.  Halleen. For many years, Ms. Halleen has dedicated her time, energy,  and talent to strengthening the lives of individuals and families  throughout our community. Her unwavering commitment to service,  advocacy, and mentorship reflects the very spirit of the Heroes Among  Us Congressional Award.

Ms. Halleen has devoted herself to creating opportunities for growth,  support, and empowerment. Through her volunteer work and leadership in  local organizations, she has ensured that community members--from  children to seniors--have access to programs, resources, and guidance  that improve their well-being. She consistently demonstrates the values  of compassion, integrity, and collaboration, bringing together  volunteers, stakeholders, and local leaders to address challenges and  create meaningful impact. Her work is characterized by hands-on  involvement and a personal touch. Ms. Halleen actively participates in  program planning, community outreach, and volunteer coordination,  always leading by example. Her dedication has strengthened initiatives  that provide critical support, educational opportunities, and  enrichment programs for those who need it most. Colleagues and  community members describe her as a tireless advocate, a connector of  people, and a source of encouragement and inspiration. Ms. Halleen's  efforts have not only addressed immediate needs but have also created  sustainable solutions that benefit the community for years to come.  Through her vision, leadership, and commitment to others, she has  helped countless individuals find hope, resources, and a stronger sense  of belonging. Her service ensures that our community continues to  thrive and that every person has an opportunity to succeed.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms.  Halleen for her extraordinary contributions to our community. For her  selfless dedication, impactful leadership, and unwavering commitment to  helping others, I am proud to honor Halleen as a 2026 Heroes Among Us  recipient for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
