Recognizing Ms. Patsy Geiger


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701428/recognizing-ms-patsy-geiger


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my  outstanding constituents, Ms. Patsy Geiger, a dedicated volunteer whose  energy, warmth, and unwavering commitment have made a remarkable impact  through Seniors in Service AmeriCorps Seniors RSVP Pasco. At 83 years  old, Patsy brings vitality, compassion, and a tireless work ethic to  every role she undertakes. Since joining Seniors in Service in 2022,  she has become a steady and trusted presence both at the One Stop Shop  Food Pantry in Dade City and throughout our volunteer community. Week  after week, she checks in clients and welcomes each person with a  genuine smile, ensuring that visitors facing challenging circumstances  feel seen, respected, and cared for. Her kindness transforms the  atmosphere of the pantry, leaving clients with more than just food-- they leave with dignity and hope.

What makes Patsy especially remarkable is her steadfast dedication,  even while managing her own health challenges. During busy months, she  contributes over 60 volunteer hours--a remarkable commitment at any  age, and truly extraordinary for someone over 80. Patsy shows up not  because it is easy, but because she believes in serving others. Beyond  her regular shifts, she actively participates in Seniors in Service  events, supports broader community initiatives, and recently stepped  into a leadership role as an RSVP Ambassador. In this role, Patsy  exemplifies the natural leadership, consistency, and encouragement that  her fellow volunteers admire. True to her adventurous spirit, Patsy is  a self-described daredevil who has skydived over 500 times--proof that  she has never been afraid to jump in and give life her all. That same  fearless energy defines her volunteer service, inspiring everyone  around her and leavIng a lasting impact on the community she serves.

For her dedication, compassion, and remarkable example, I am proud to  recognize Ms. Patsy Geiger as a 2026 Hero Among Us. I ask my colleagues  to join me in honoring her extraordinary service, her fearless spirit,  and the lasting difference she has made in the lives of countless  neighbors.
