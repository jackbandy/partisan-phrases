Recognizing Ms. Chris Hogarth


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701143/recognizing-ms-chris-hogarth


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding  contributions of a dedicated volunteer in my district, Ms. Chris  Hogarth. Chris has been an invaluable volunteer at the ReStores,  dedicating her time, energy, and organizational skills to support the  community and ensure the stores operate efficiently. Chris has taken  exceptional care of the book room, keeping it organized and welcoming  for shoppers. She has also overseen the games and toys section,  carefully sorting and displaying items to make them accessible and  enjoyable for families. In addition, Chris has played a crucial role in  managing all holiday donations, helping to bring seasonal joy and  support to the community.

Her consistent commitment, attention to detail, and tireless service  have made a meaningful impact on the ReStores' operations and the  customers they serve. Chris Hogarth exemplifies the spirit of  volunteerism and selfless service that strengthens our communities and  touches countless lives.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms. Chris  Hogarth for her outstanding contributions and dedication to serving  others. For her generosity, commitment, and exemplary service, I am  proud to honor Ms. Hogarth as a 2026 Heroes Among Us recipient for  Florida's 12th Congressional District.
