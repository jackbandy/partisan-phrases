Recognizing Pastor Freddie Hinson, Jr.


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700362/recognizing-pastor-freddie-hinson-jr


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  extraordinary contributions of a dedicated community leader in my  district, Pastor Freddie Hinson, Jr. Pastor Hinson has been a devoted  member, volunteer, and Spiritual Advisor to the YMCA Togetherhood  Program, where his guidance and leadership have made a transformative  impact in one of our community's most underserved areas.

Pastor Hinson's service goes far beyond ceremonial duties. He  actively mentors leaders, encourages volunteers, and helps shape  initiatives that address food insecurity, health disparities,  neighborhood revitalization, and family empowerment. Through prayer,  strategic insight, and hands-on participation, he brings both spiritual  grounding and practical leadership to every effort, ensuring that the  YMCA Togetherhood Program's initiatives are not only meaningful but  sustainable. A native of Clearwater, Florida, Pastor Hinson accepted  his call to public ministry while serving in the U.S. Army at SHAPE,  Belgium, where he served as a Soldier, Minister, and Musician. Upon  returning home, he was licensed as a minister and faithfully served in  leadership roles for decades, including as Senior Pastor of Union  Baptist Church in Port Richey and currently as Senior Pastor of New  Hope Baptist Church of Hudson, a position he has held for 28 years.  Beyond his ministry, Pastor Hinson has strengthened partnerships  between faith communities, civic leaders, and grassroots organizations.  He served as Director of the City of Clearwater's Neighborhood  Services Department, where he worked to promote neighborhood  sustainability and urban redevelopment. His efforts have mobilized  community volunteers, fostered unity, and created lasting positive  change in the lives of residents throughout Hernando County. Pastor  Hinson's life reflects a commitment to service, family, and faith.  Married for forty years, he is a proud father of two daughters and  grandfather of two granddaughters. His devotion to family mirrors his  dedication to ministry and community, demonstrating a life grounded in  integrity, servant leadership, and lasting impact.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Pastor  Freddie Hinson, Jr. for his decades of faithful service, leadership,  and transformative contributions to our community. For his  extraordinary commitment to strengthening both church and community, I  am proud to honor Pastor Hinson as a 2026 Heroes Among Us recipient for  Florida's 12th Congressional District.
