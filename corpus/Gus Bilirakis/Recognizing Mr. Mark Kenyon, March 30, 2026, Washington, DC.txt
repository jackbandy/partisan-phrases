Recognizing Mr. Mark Kenyon


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701526/recognizing-mr-mark-kenyon


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an  extraordinary servant leader in my district, Mr. Mark Kenyon, whose  dedication to others exemplifies the very best of our community. Mark  embodies the spirit of service that defines the Community Service  Council of West Pasco County, and his reliability, compassion, and  hands-on leadership have made a lasting impact on countless families.  Mark is involved in nearly every service project undertaken by the  Council, consistently stepping forward with a willingness to help and a  positive attitude that inspires everyone around him. His commitment is  not occasional--it is constant. When there is work to be done, Mark is  there, ready to serve. Among his many contributions, Mark's leadership  of the Home Spruce Up Program stands out as a shining example of  volunteerism at its finest. This initiative identifies homes in our  community in need of non-structural repairs--projects that can  dramatically improve safety, dignity, and quality of life for residents  who may not have the financial means or physical ability to complete  them on their own. As an experienced handyman, Mark plays a critical  leadership role, carefully assessing each project site, determining  necessary materials, estimating quantities, and projecting realistic  timelines to ensure the work is done efficiently and effectively. Mark  approaches each home with empathy and respect. He takes the time to  understand the homeowner's needs and thoughtfully organizes volunteers  to address them with care. Whether repairing steps, painting, clearing  yards, or making small but meaningful improvements, Mark ensures that  every project reflects quality craftsmanship and genuine compassion.  His ability to coordinate teams, solve problems on the spot, and keep  morale high has transformed the Home Spruce Up Program into one of the  Council's most successful and cherished efforts.

What truly sets Mark apart is his heart. He treats every homeowner  with dignity, listening to their stories and ensuring they feel seen  and supported. Volunteers consistently share that Mark's leadership  elevates the entire experience--he leads not by title, but by example.  He arrives early, stays late, and never hesitates to take on the  toughest tasks himself. Though he rarely seeks recognition, his impact  is unmistakable. Beyond the projects he leads, Mark is the person the  organization can depend on for nearly any event or initiative. His  consistency and dependability have strengthened programs across the  board and enabled the Council to serve more families than ever before.

Mr. Speaker, for his unwavering dedication, steady leadership, and  the countless hours he has invested in improving the lives of others, I  am proud to recognize Mark Kenyon as a 2026 Heroes Among Us recipient  for Florida's 12th Congressional District. His service reflects the  very best of our community, and I ask my colleagues to join me in  honoring his extraordinary contributions.
