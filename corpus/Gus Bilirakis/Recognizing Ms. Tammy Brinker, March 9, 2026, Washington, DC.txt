Recognizing Ms. Tammy Brinker


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700611/recognizing-ms-tammy-brinker


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor Ms.  Tammy Brinker for her extraordinary leadership and the transformative  impact she has made during more than a decade of service as Chief  Executive Officer of her organization.

Through visionary leadership, strategic foresight, and an unwavering  commitment to students and educators, Ms. Brinker guided the  organization from an annual revenue of less than $100,000 to a  thriving, mission-driven nonprofit generating more than $3 million  annually. Under her stewardship, more than $40 million has been raised  to support schools, teachers, and students--all while maintaining the  highest standards of financial integrity, transparency, and  sustainability. Ms. Brinker's legacy is defined not only by growth, but  by meaningful impact. She has been instrumental in launching  innovative, high-impact programs designed to remove barriers and meet  real, tangible needs within our community. The Free Tools 4 Schools  Store and Mobile Store have provided teachers with free classroom  supplies, directly supporting those who dedicate their lives to  educating the next generation. Through Operation Cinderella Boutique,  she ensured that students could participate in milestone events with  dignity and confidence, reinforcing that no child should miss out on  life's important moments due to financial hardship. Her leadership also  strengthened mentoring and scholarship initiatives such as Take Stock  in Children, expanding access to academic support, college readiness  resources, and postsecondary opportunities. By broadening these  pathways, she helped countless students envision--and achieve--brighter  futures.

Beyond programmatic success, Ms. Brinker elevated governance and  operational excellence. She cultivated a highly engaged Board of  Directors and implemented sound strategic and financial practices that  earned the trust and confidence of donors, partners, and the broader  community. Her approach reflects not only competence, but character-- ensuring that growth was both responsible and sustainable.

Mr. Speaker, Tammy Brinker's leadership has profoundly shaped both  the organization she served and the countless lives it touches. Her  vision, integrity, and steadfast dedication exemplify the very best of  community-focused leadership. For these reasons, I am proud to  recognize Ms. Tammy Brinker as a 2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th  Congressional District.
