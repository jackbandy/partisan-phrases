Recognizing Ms. Lisa Campos


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701533/recognizing-ms-lisa-campos


Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an exceptional  leader, devoted volunteer, and joyful servant of our community, Ms.  Lisa Campos. Lisa is one of the most dependable and inspiring forces  within the Community Service Council of West Pasco. Over the years, she  has faithfully served in multiple leadership roles, including Vice  President and now President. In every capacity, she has led with  humility, enthusiasm, and a steadfast commitment to strengthening our  community. Her leadership style is rooted in collaboration and  kindness--she brings people together, motivates volunteers, and ensures  that each project is carried out with care, purpose, and heart. One of  the clearest examples of Lisa's character is found in the Council's  largest annual fundraising event, a high-energy cooking competition  inspired by the show ``Chopped.'' While the event draws excitement and  applause, it also leaves behind a mountain of dishes and cleanup. Year  after year, Lisa quietly steps away from the spotlight to take on one  of the most grueling and unglamorous tasks--washing the dishes. She  does so without hesitation, without complaint, and always with a smile.  Her willingness to embrace the dirtiest job perfectly reflects her  servant's heart. She leads not by title, but by example. Lisa's  commitment to service extends far beyond the Council. She is a  published children's book author whose work is available in both  English and Spanish, demonstrating her creativity and her desire to  reach and uplift children and families across cultures. She and her  husband have also opened their home for many years to foreign exchange  students, providing young people from around the world with a safe,  welcoming, and nurturing environment in which to learn and grow. In  addition, Lisa volunteers her time by bringing her pet dog to senior  citizen events, offering companionship, comfort, and joy to seniors who  may otherwise feel isolated. These simple but meaningful visits  brighten lives, spark connection, and remind our older neighbors that  they are seen and valued. What truly sets Lisa apart is her spirit. She  is cheerful, positive, and always ready to step in wherever help is  needed--whether organizing an event, encouraging a fellow volunteer, or  filling a last-minute gap. Her presence uplifts every room she enters,  and her reliability makes her someone the community can always count  on.

Mr. Speaker, for her leadership, her generosity, and her unwavering  commitment to serving others, I am proud to recognize Lisa Campos as a  2026 Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District. Her  contributions have strengthened the Community Service Council of West  Pasco and enriched the lives of countless individuals throughout our  district. She exemplifies what it means to serve with humility,  compassion, and joy.
