Welcoming Pastor Aaron Derstine


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1702154/welcoming-pastor-aaron-derstine


Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to welcome and thank our  guest chaplain, Pastor Aaron Derstine for offering today's opening  prayer.

Pastor Derstine currently serves as the senior pastor for Hopewell  Christian Fellowship in Telford, Pennsylvania. He received his academic  and ministry training at Wheaton College before entering full-time  ministry in 2002 and continues to serve as a staff writer with Eagles'  Wings Ministries in Buffalo, New York.

For many years, Pastor Derstine has devoted himself to studying and  teaching the Word of God, speaking at churches and events both here at  home and abroad.

He is also a gifted musician and a worship leader, using his musical  talents across the country and internationally, including work amongst  Jewish and Arab communities in Israel and the broader Middle East.

Mr. Speaker, I am grateful for Pastor Derstine's prayer this morning.  We thank him for his faithful ministry. I thank Pastor Derstine, and  may God bless him and his continued service.
