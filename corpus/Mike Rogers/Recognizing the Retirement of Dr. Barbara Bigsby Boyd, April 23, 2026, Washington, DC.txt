Recognizing the Retirement of Dr. Barbara Bigsby Boyd


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1702190/recognizing-the-retirement-of-dr-barbara-bigsby-boyd


Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and  honor Dr. Barbara Bigsby Boyd. Dr. Boyd was elected in 1994 to the  Alabama House of Representatives for the 32nd District covering  portions of Calhoun and Talladega Counties. She has served as the  Democratic Caucus Vice Chair and a member of the National Black Caucus  of State Legislators.

Dr. Boyd was born in Anniston, Alabama, on January 31, 1937. She  attended Cobb High School and received her B.A. Degree in French and  English from Miles College, her M.A. in Supervision and Curriculum  Development, and her Ed.D. in Instructional Leadership and Reading from  the University of Alabama.

Dr. Boyd spent 45 years in education serving in both public and  private education in secondary, two-year and four-year colleges  including Jacksonville State University, Gadsden State Community  College, and Miles College.

Dr. Boyd is a member of the Trustee Board of Murray Temple Christian  Methodist Episcopal Church, a life member of the NAACP, as well as the  Alabama New South Coalition and Dorcas Art and Social Club. She is also  a member of the Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc., Phi Delta Kappa, and  Kappa Delta Pi. Her committees in the State House include Children and  Senior Advocacy, County and Municipal Government, Rules and State  Government.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing and  celebrating the extraordinary career of Dr. Boyd and wish her the very  best in her retirement. I thank her for her service to the state of  Alabama.
