Recognizing the Oxford High School Basketball Team for Winning the Ahsaa Class 6A State Championship


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700800/recognizing-the-oxford-high-school-basketball-team-for-winning-the-ahsaa-class-6a-state-championship


Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the  Oxford High School boys' basketball team for winning the AHSAA 6A State  Title. The team's record is 30-4 and is coached by Joel Van Meter.

On Saturday, March 7, 2026, at the Birmingham Jefferson Convention  Center's Legacy Arena, the Oxford High School team beat the Cullman  High School team 68 to 44. Tournament MVP Jaylen Alexander scored 11  points, had eight assists, and had five steals. Chris Latson scored 21  points, Marcus Perry, Jr. scored 17 points, and Caileb Wilson scored 10  points. This is Oxford High School's first basketball state title in  school history.

Mr. Speaker, please join me in celebration as we congratulate the  team, coaches, students, faculty and fans at Oxford High School on this  exciting occasion. Go Big O.
