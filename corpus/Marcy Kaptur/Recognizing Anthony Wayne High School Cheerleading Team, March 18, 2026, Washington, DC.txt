Recognizing Anthony Wayne High School Cheerleading Team


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700850/recognizing-anthony-wayne-high-school-cheerleading-team


Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the extraordinary  young women of the Anthony Wayne High School cheer team.

Just weeks after capturing the National Cheer Championship title in  Orlando, Florida, these remarkable athletes brought home the Ohio  Association of Secondary School Administrators State Championship  title.

Led by Erica Black and Robin Buffy, the Generals had the highest  score across all teams in all divisions.

Championships are not handed out. They are earned with early  mornings, late nights, and countless unseen hours of practice. They are  built by coaches who mentor, parents who sacrifice, teachers who  encourage, and neighbors who show up again and again.

This victory belongs to the entire Anthony Wayne community. It  reflects the strength of our schools, the spirit of our families, and  the promise of our young people. Our champions have inspired us and  reminded us what teamwork, perseverance, and hometown pride can  achieve.

I congratulate them, and Go Generals.
