Recognizing Students At Patrick Henry Middle School


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701866/recognizing-students-at-patrick-henry-middle-school


Mr. ESPAILLAT. Mr. Speaker, last week, I had the privilege of helping  students and staff at the Patrick Henry Middle School by securing  $500,000 to renovate the school. Actually, Mr. Speaker, this was an  effort led by students, which exemplifies civic duty advocacy on behalf  of their school and their community.

While there, I was also able to meet students that helped advocate  for this project, including the student council copresidents, Doris  Espinoza and Thomas Stevens; the student council vice president, Jade  Perez; and the other members of the council: Humayra Chowdhury,  Victoria Quinonez, Sadie Metz, Chloe Manning, Mujahidul Islam, Alessa  Rodriguez, Emma Van-Eiker, Rosalie McCall-Feighery, Abby Maria- Catalino, and Kylie Cruz, great students that through their advocacy  brought a renovation to their school.

I want to acknowledge the principal of the school who has done also a  tremendous job, but first and foremost, student-led power, showing that  they can do it for their school and their community.

I am proud to have helped secure more than $14 million for my  district, and I look forward to helping fund initiatives that will  support the needs of New York-13.
