Recognizing Mr. Don Laffoon's Retirement After 47 Years of Service to Electrical Corporation of America


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1702192/recognizing-mr-don-laffoons-retirement-after-47-years-of-service-to-electrical-corporation-of-america


Mr. ALFORD. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize a remarkable  Missouri business leader and true American success story--Mr. Don  Laffoon of Raytown, Missouri--as he begins a well-earned retirement.

Don is the longtime President and CEO, and now Chairman of the Board,  of Electrical Corporation of America, a Raytown-based company that has  grown into a nationally respected contractor operating in more than 40  states, as well as Mexico and Canada. Under his leadership, the company  has delivered complex industrial and power projects while maintaining a  strong commitment to safety, quality, and integrity.

What makes Don's story especially meaningful is how it began. He  joined the company in 1979 as a warehouseman and driver, completed an  electrical apprenticeship, became a journeyman wireman, and worked his  way up through nearly every role in the business.

In 2003, he and his partners purchased the company and continued  building a strong operation that creates good-paying jobs and supports  critical infrastructure.

Don has also devoted decades of service to the National Electrical  Contractors Association, recently serving as Chairman of its Government  Affairs Committee, advocating for contractors and workforce development  nationwide.

Mr. Speaker, on behalf of Missouri's 4th Congressional. District, I  thank Don Laffoon for his lifetime of service and wish him continued  health and happiness in retirement.
