Constituent of the Week: Sheriff Scott Foster


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701023/constituent-of-the-week-sheriff-scott-foster


Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sheriff Scott  Foster for his 31 years of outstanding service in law enforcement.

Sheriff Foster began his career in 1994, advancing from the rank of  deputy to captain. On September 15, 2022, he was appointed sheriff of  the city of Fredericksburg and was sworn in shortly afterwards.

Since taking office, Sheriff Foster has worked to improve the  Fredericksburg Sheriff's Office by updating operational plans,  implementing policies and procedures, and improving training and  recordkeeping.

Under his leadership, he also established a career development  program to support employee growth and recognize professional  excellence within the department.

I personally congratulate and thank Sheriff Foster for his dedication  to ensuring our Fredericksburg communities stay safe and have access to  the resources they need. His impact on community will be felt for years  to come.
