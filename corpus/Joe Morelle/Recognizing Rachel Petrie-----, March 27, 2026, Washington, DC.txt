Recognizing Rachel Petrie-----


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701525/recognizing-rachel-petrie


Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today during Women's History Month  to honor an exceptional and influential leader from Rochester, New  York: Rachel Petrie.

Rachel is a dedicated nurse whose service exemplifies the very best  of our healthcare professionals. For more than seven years at Unity  Hospital, she has served in the Emergency Department in a variety of  critical roles, including Charge Nurse, Triage Nurse, EMS Triage Nurse,  Preceptor, and Mentor. In each capacity, she demonstrates exceptional  skill, steady leadership, and an unwavering commitment to patient care.

Her impact extends beyond clinical excellence. Rachel strengthens her  team through mentorship and collaboration, helping to foster  confidence, cohesion, and resilience in a fast-paced and demanding  environment. Outside of the hospital, she continues her service by  volunteering with her local EMS team and Fire Department, further  demonstrating her dedication to helping others.

Mr. Speaker, Rachel Petrie's work reflects a deep and enduring  commitment to service, compassion, and community. During Women's  History Month, it is especially fitting that we honor extraordinary  women who have gone above and beyond to strengthen our communities. I  am proud to recognize her and extend my deepest gratitude for her  continued service.
