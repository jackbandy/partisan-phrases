Recognizing Nija Branca Bilofsky


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701495/recognizing-nija-branca-bilofsky


Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today during Women's History Month  to honor an exceptional and influential leader from Rochester, New  York: Nija Branca Bilofsky.

Nija has devoted her career to education and to inspiring the next  generation of students. Over nearly three decades in the classroom,  including many years at the School of the Arts, she has taught ninth  and tenth grade English with a commitment to both academic excellence  and student growth. Her teaching fosters critical thinking, creativity,  and confidence, empowering students to find their voice and reach their  full potential.

Beyond her work with students, Nija is a trusted and supportive  colleague who consistently uplifts those around her. Her dedication to  her school community, paired with her compassion and professionalism,  makes her an invaluable presence both inside and outside the classroom.

Mr. Speaker, Nija Branca Bilofsky's career reflects a deep and  enduring commitment to education, mentorship, and community. During  Women's History Month, it is especially fitting that we honor  extraordinary women who have gone above and beyond to strengthen our  communities. I am proud to recognize her and extend my deepest  gratitude for her continued service.
