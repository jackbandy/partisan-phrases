Recognizing the Rochester Olympians


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701538/recognizing-the-rochester-olympians


Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the incredible  Olympians from the  Rochester community who represented Team USA at the 2026 Winter  Olympics: Dylan Walczyk, Christopher Lillis, and Haley Winn. These  exceptional athletes proudly represented our region on the world stage,  competing with determination and excellence at the Winter Games last  month. Their achievements reflect the strong tradition of athletic  dedication and excellence that our community has long fostered.

Our local athletes demonstrated their remarkable abilities in various  Olympic events. From the slopes to the rink, these competitors faced  the very best athletes from around the world. Two of Rochester's own  reached the top of the podium and brought home Olympic gold medals.  Haley Winn competed in women's ice hockey and helped lead the team to a  gold medal victory, defeating Canada in the championship game.  Christopher Lillis competed in the freestyle skiing mixed team aerials  competition, securing himself a second gold medal after winning the  same event at the 2022 Winter Olympics.

Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the entire Rochester community, I  congratulate these Olympians on their extraordinary athletic  achievements at the 2026 Winter Olympics. We are incredibly proud of  their remarkable accomplishments and contributions to inspiring the  next generation of athletes in our region and across the country.
