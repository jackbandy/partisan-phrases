Recognizing Dr. Michelle E. Richards in Honor of Women's History Month


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701712/recognizing-dr-michelle-e-richards-in-honor-of-womens-history-month


Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr.  Michelle E. Richards in Honor of Women's History Month. I include in  the Record the following proclamation:

Whereas, Women's History Month is a time to recognize and       celebrate the extraordinary contributions of women who have       shaped communities, inspired generations, and demonstrated       unwavering leadership, compassion, and excellence; and

Whereas, Dr. Michelle E. Richards is a Jamaican-born       evangelist, humanitarian, entrepreneur, and visionary leader       whose life's work reflects a steadfast commitment to faith,       service, and empowerment; and

Whereas, Dr. Richards received her early education at Holy       Childhood High School and later earned a Bachelor's Degree       from the University of Technology in Kingston, Jamaica,       before relocating to the United States in 1999 and continuing       a distinguished professional career in the banking industry;       and

Whereas, answering her divine calling to ministry, Dr.       Richards was baptized in 2003, ordained in 2006, and later       established Visions International Ministries, a global       ministry that has impacted lives through preaching,       mentorship, humanitarian outreach, and purpose-driven       initiatives across multiple nations; and

Whereas, through her humanitarian work and global outreach,       Dr. Richards has provided food, clothing, school supplies,       and essential resources to communities throughout the       Caribbean, Africa, and beyond, while leading mission efforts       that uplift individuals and families in need; and

Whereas, her influence as a preacher, motivational speaker,       humanitarian, and community leader continues to inspire       individuals around the world to pursue faith, purpose, and       service while uplifting others through compassion and       leadership; and therefore, be it

Resolved, That we, Florida's 20th Congressional District,       proudly recognize and honor Dr. Michelle E. Richards during       Women's History Month for her outstanding leadership,       dedication, and service, inspiring others through her example       of faith, compassion, and achievement.
