Recognizing Rutshelle Guillaume in Honor of Women's History Month


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700908/recognizing-rutshelle-guillaume-in-honor-of-womens-history-month


Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize  Rutshelle Guillaume in honor of Women's History Month. I include in the  Record the following proclamation in her honor.

Whereas, Rutshelle Guillaume is a Haitian singer and       songwriter whose powerful voice, captivating performances,       and unique musical talent have made her a leading figure in       Haitian music; and

Whereas, born and raised in Haiti in a musical family,       Rutshelle cultivated her passion for singing at a young age,       performing at local events and talent competitions, and       developing the artistry that would define her career; and

Whereas, over the course of her career, Rutshelle has       released multiple albums showcasing her distinctive style,       upbeat energy, and inspiring messages, resonating with       audiences across Haiti and internationally; and

Whereas, Rutshelle has earned a reputation as a dynamic       performer, captivating audiences with her live performances       and inspiring others through her dedication to her craft and       her commitment to creating meaningful music; and

Whereas, beyond her musical achievements, Rutshelle is a       passionate advocate for social causes, using her platform to       raise awareness and make a positive impact in her community,       both in Haiti and abroad; and

Whereas, her remarkable achievements have earned her       numerous awards and accolades, including recognition as one       of the top female artists in Haiti, while her humility, work       ethic, and dedication continue to inspire fans and fellow       artists alike; and therefore, be it

Resolved, that we, Florida's 20th Congressional District,       proudly recognize and honor Rutshelle Guillaume during       Women's History Month for her exceptional talent, dedication,       and contributions to music and the community, inspiring       others through her example of artistry, perseverance, and       leadership,
