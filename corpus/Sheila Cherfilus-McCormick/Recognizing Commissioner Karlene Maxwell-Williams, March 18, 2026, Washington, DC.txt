Recognizing Commissioner Karlene Maxwell-Williams


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700924/recognizing-commissioner-karlene-maxwell-williams


Mrs. Cherfilus-McCormick. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize  Commissioner Karlene Maxwell-Williams of Lauderdale Lakes, Florida, and  include in the Record the following proclamation in her honor:

Whereas, Commissioner Karlene Maxwell-Williams, originally       from Kingston. Jamaica, immigrated to the United States as a       teenager and has since made Lauderdale Lakes, Florida her       home for over two decades; and

Whereas, Commissioner Maxwell-Williams exemplifies the       values of perseverance, dedication, and service, having       worked for 15 years with the Florida Department of Revenue in       Child Support Enforcement, founded her own Mediation and       Divorce business, and currently serving as a Florida Supreme       Court Certified Mediator and volunteer judge for the Broward       County Teen Court; and

Whereas, Commissioner Maxwell-Williams is a Department of       Justice Accredited Representative and Citizenship Coordinator       for the Florida Immigrant Coalition, providing immigration       legal services and organizing citizenship drives to empower       new Americans; and

Whereas, she has devoted herself to community service       through mentorship, feeding programs for the homeless, youth       initiatives,       and volunteer leadership with the Kiwanis Club of Lauderdale       Lakes and Broward County Public Schools; and

Whereas, Commissioner Maxwell-Williams was elected to the       Lauderdale Lakes City Commission in November 2020, has served       as Vice Mayor, Chair of the Community Redevelopment Agency,       and Delegate to the National League of Cities, and continues       to promote equity, diversity, and inclusion throughout her       community; and

Whereas, Commissioner Maxwell-Williams holds several       leadership roles, including Vice-Chair of the NLC Race,       Equity, and Leadership (REAL) Council, 2nd Vice-Chair of the       Broward County Commission on the Status of Women, Director of       the Broward League of Cities, and Delegate for the Florida       League of Cities; and

Whereas, Commissioner Maxwell-Williams is a devoted mother,       wife, and community leader whose personal motto, ``Taking       Action, Getting Results,'' reflects her unwavering commitment       to service, empowerment, and positive change; and

Therefore, be it resolved that we, Florida's 20th       Congressional District, proudly recognize and honor       Commissioner Karlene Maxwell-Williams during Women's History       Month, for her outstanding leadership, dedication, and       service to the community, inspiring others through her       example of integrity, compassion, and achievement.
