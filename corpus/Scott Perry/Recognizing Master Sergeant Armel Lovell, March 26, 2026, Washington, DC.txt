Recognizing Master Sergeant Armel Lovell


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701378/recognizing-master-sergeant-armel-lovell


Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Master Sergeant  Armel Lovell upon the auspicious occasion of his retirement from the  Pennsylvania Air National Guard after 23 years of selfless service.

Master Sergeant Lovell served his entire career in 211th Engineering  Installation Squadron. Some of his myriad job responsibilities within  Cyber Transport Systems included deploying, sustaining,  troubleshooting, and repairing standard voice, data and video network  infrastructure systems, IP detection systems, and cryptographic  equipment. Additionally, he managed devices and systems with the most  current software tools and implemented and maintained all aspects of  network systems, cryptographic equipment, and video services using  technical data, voltage, waveform measurements, and block diagrams.

At the time of his retirement, Master Sergeant Lovell served as the  Non-Commissioned Officer in Charge of the Cyber Transport Unit. His  work protecting and strengthening the Air Force's communications  network helped keep his fellow Airmen and our Nation safe.

Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize, commend, and congratulate  Master Sergeant Armel Lovell on his retirement upon more than 23 years  of selfless service in the Pennsylvania Air National Guard, and on a  job extremely well done. His dedication, professionalism, and tireless  devotion are a great credit to himself, his fellow Airmen, the  Pennsylvania National Guard, the United States Air Force, and the  United States of America.
