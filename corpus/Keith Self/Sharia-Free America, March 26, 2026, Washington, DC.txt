Sharia-Free America

Mr. SELF. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with my colleagues from the  Sharia-Free America Caucus, which my good friend and fellow Texan  Congressman Chip Roy and I proudly co-chair.

Just today, we added our 60th member. I am delighted to announce that  the caucus now has representation from 25 States across the United  States, which is half of all the States in this Union.

America was founded on a timeless principle, individual freedom  secured by self-government under our clear and supreme rule of  constitutional law.

As John Adams warned: ``Our Constitution was made only for a moral  and religious people. It is wholly inadequate to the government of any  other.''

Today, we witness the painful truth of those words. Multitudes arrive  at our borders with no intention of assimilating into our culture or  embracing the founding principles of liberty under the U.S.  Constitution.

The clash is not new. In 1786, Thomas Jefferson and John Adams asked  the Ambassador from Tripoli why his nation attacked peaceful countries  with no provocation. He replied that it was their right and duty under  their laws of the prophet and the Koran to make war on all who do not  acknowledge Islamic authority to enslave prisoners and that every  fighter slain would go to paradise.

This was then and remains today a stark contrast of civilizations,  one rooted in submission and domination, the other in individual  liberty under constitutional law.

Sharia poses a unique and profound danger. It is far more than a  personal faith. It is a comprehensive system of governance, culture,  and domination that enforces submission through violence.

All cultures are not morally equivalent. Whereas, Christianity is the  religion of peace, reconciliation, and love, Islam is a religion of the  sword. It stands in direct irreconcilable conflict with a maximum  individual freedom at the heart of Western civilization and our Judeo- Christian heritage.

Our rights derive from our creator, not from any man-made supremacist  code. America gladly welcomes those who come to assimilate and worship  freely according to conscience, but we must never accommodate a system  that demands the submission of every surrounding society. And sharia  explicitly requires precisely that.

The evidence is mounting right here in my home State, the Lone Star  State. In my own Congressional District, Texas' Third District, the  East Plano Islamic Center, one of the largest mosques in north Texas,  already operates a sharia-adherent enclave in Plano. This is not merely  a house of worship. It serves as the foundation for something far more  troubling.

The same organization spearheaded a proposed 400-plus acre  residential development in unincorporated Collin and Hunt Counties near  Josephine originally called EPIC City now rebranded as The Meadow to  deflect scrutiny and confusion over its separatist intent.

Marketed as a Muslim-centric community, it was designed around  sharia-adherent living, over 1,000 homes, a new mosque, a K-12 school,  and other facilities tailored to enforce submission rather than  assimilation.

Both the existing EPIC enclave and this affiliated project are  abominations that threaten American principles of equality, individual  liberty, and the rule of law.

We have seen alarming incidents of Muslim intimidation. An imam in  Dallas called the police on a fellow Muslim for merely speaking to a  Christian pastor. Another in Houston captured on video threatening to  occupy noncompliant businesses under sharia.

Texas is seeing explosive growth with roughly 48 new mosques in just  the last 2 years.

That is why on March 3, Texas Republicans overwhelmingly passed  proposition 10 with 95 percent support, a clear mandate to ban sharia  in our State.

Texans have spoken: American law only. No foreign ideology should  make Americans feel like foreigners in their own country. Texas stands  as ground zero in this fight. Yet, this is not just a Texas issue.  Sharia's influence is advancing from Arizona to Minnesota to Alabama,  Florida, and beyond. No State is immune. That is why our caucus has  surged to 60 members across 25 States bolstered by strong Senate  support from figures like Senator Tuberville.

As I have said repeatedly, sharia has no place in America. Do not  sharia our America. Defend the West. Ban sharia.

The urgency of this fight was laid bare in the opening days of March  when a disturbing wave of Islamist-inspired attacks struck our soil. On  March 1, in Austin, a gunman opened fire outside a downtown bar,  killing three innocent young Americans and wounding more than a dozen  others. The Islamist terrorist, a naturalized citizen from Senegal with  prior arrests, wore a sweatshirt proclaiming: ``Property of Allah,'' an  undershirt bearing an Iranian flag, and left a Koran in his vehicle.

This was no isolated act.

March has seen multiple incidents linked to Islamist extremism: a  failed ISIS-inspired bombing attempt outside Gracie Mansion in New York  City on March 7; a vehicle ramming and a shootout at a synagogue in  Michigan on March 12, injuring dozens; and an ISIS-inspired shooting at  Old Dominion University in Virginia that same day, targeting ROTC  members and claiming one life.

These attacks, clustered in a single month, underscore a chilling  reality: The enemy is inside the gate, driven by a supremacist ideology  that claims divine authority over our Constitution and justifies  violence against those who refuse submission.

Nowhere in this ideology is it more barbaric than its treatment of  women. Under sharia, women are relegated to second-class status. It  explicitly permits wife beating as discipline. Imams have preached that  women feel honored by beatings, or liken them to livestock, cows,  sheep, horses, and mules, created solely for men's use.

Sharia codifies female genital mutilation, forced child marriage,  honor killings, half inheritance for women, and testimony worth half  that of a man.

Its nations enforce these rules, from Iran's reign of terror to  places where adultery brings stoning, apostasy brings death or public  floggings, and men and women live in slavery.

Even more horrifying, sharia permits the rape and enslavement of non- Muslim women, with some scholars openly declaring it permissible under  the Koran and hadith. These are not fringe opinions. They are preached  publicly, and they are infiltrating Western Civilization.

If you don't believe this is happening, Mr. Speaker, then tell that  to the thousands of British teenage girls groomed, plied with alcohol,  gang-raped by Muslim gangs, and then arrested by police for being drunk  while their rapists go free.

Let me repeat: The victims are arrested. This is sharia. Sharia does  not content itself with private practice. It demands eventual dominance  over the host culture. Look at France and the U.K., Mr. Speaker, where  unchecked immigration has eroded heritage, culture, and freedoms. We  cannot allow that fate here in the United States.

According to Islam, many non-Muslims like me are regarded as  infidels, and Muslims who convert to Christianity or any other faith  are apostates, punishable by death under Islam.

As Karl Popper wisely cautioned: ``Unlimited tolerance must lead to  the disappearance of tolerance.'' Mr. Speaker, we must claim the right  to not tolerate the intolerant.

Sharia embodies that intolerance, rejecting free speech, equal  rights, and constitutional supremacy. We must reject it in turn.

Legislation alone cannot halt this creeping threat. Congress has a  sworn duty to act. Seven bills stand ready to ban sharia's influence  regarding immigration and legal systems, deporting adherents who  promote it, designating the Muslim Brotherhood and CAIR as terrorist  organizations, strengthening vetting, advancing the PAUSE Act to stop  all immigration, and ensuring no court ever considers sharia or any  foreign religious code.

Mr. Speaker, the American people demand that we defend the  Constitution against ideology seeking to supplant it and replace it.  That is why Congressman Roy and I founded this caucus. It has grown  rapidly because patriots nationwide are awakening.

Mr. Speaker, I urge every colleague, Republican or Democrat, who  believes in American sovereignty to join the Sharia-Free America Caucus  today. Let us stand firm: one nation, one Constitution, and one rule of  law.

Sharia has no place in America.
