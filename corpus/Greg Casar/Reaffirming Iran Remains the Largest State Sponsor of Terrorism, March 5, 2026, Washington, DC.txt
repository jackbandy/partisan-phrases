Reaffirming Iran Remains the Largest State Sponsor of Terrorism


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700563/reaffirming-iran-remains-the-largest-state-sponsor-of-terrorism


Mr. CASAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise in opposition to H. Res. 1099. While I  unambiguously condemn the brutal, repressive Iranian regime, I will  vote against this resolution because I fear it will be used as a  pretext to support Trump's illegal war of choice.

I oppose Trump's illegal war in Iran, which is killing U.S.  servicemembers, destabilizing the Middle East, wasting billions of  taxpayer dollars, and raising the price of gas, all while Americans are  losing their healthcare. I remain clear-eyed about the nature of the  Iranian government and condemn its malign activities across the region  and its brutal domestic repression, including its recent slaughter of  thousands of protesters. However, the appalling behavior of governments  abroad cannot give Trump the unilateral power to start forever wars  across the globe.
