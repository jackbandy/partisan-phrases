Russia's Abduction and Indoctrination of Ukrainian Children


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701464/russias-abduction-and-indoctrination-of-ukrainian-children


Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of the 35,000  children in Ukraine that Putin and others have stolen during this  unprovoked and brutal war.

Shockingly, our government just made things worse as it relates to  these children. Last week, Trump waived sanctions on Russian oil  companies, including two Russian oil companies that, based on recent  reporting, have directly funded and facilitated the abduction and  indoctrination of Ukrainian children.

Lifting the sanctions will do little to nothing to ease gas prices  but will significantly benefit these two Russian oil companies. These  companies are helping to steal children, and Trump just helped them  out. They will make billions of dollars.

In the coming days, we will work to rally Congress to compel Trump to  reverse these sanctions. Every single person responsible for stealing  Ukrainian children must be held accountable.
