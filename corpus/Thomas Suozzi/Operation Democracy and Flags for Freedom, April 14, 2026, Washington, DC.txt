Operation Democracy and Flags for Freedom


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701665/operation-democracy-and-flags-for-freedom


Mr. SUOZZI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Operation  Democracy and their Flags for Freedom program.

In my district, the hamlet of Locust Valley proudly founded Operation  Democracy's sister city program in 1947, partnering with the first  town, Normandy, France, liberated on D-day.

Now, Oyster Bay-East Norwich and Cold Spring Harbor, also in my  district, participate in the Flags for Freedom program, as well.

Operation Democracy was founded in the wake of World War II as a  humanitarian effort to rebuild war-torn cities across the globe. Since  then, it has connected more than 500 communities with partner  communities throughout the world, fostering diplomacy, peace, and  mutual understanding.

Their Flags for Freedom program builds powerful bridges between  generations. Through American Legion posts, local veterans present  American flags to graduating high school seniors.

Nearly 10,000 flags have already been presented as living lessons of  service, sacrifice, and citizenship.

I encourage every congressional district across the country to enter  into the Flags for Freedom program. It is a simple, nonpartisan tribute  to our veterans and our Nation's flag, and it is rooted in both history  and gratitude.
