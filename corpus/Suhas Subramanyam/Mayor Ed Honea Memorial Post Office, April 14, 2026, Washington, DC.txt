Mayor Ed Honea Memorial Post Office


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701654/mayor-ed-honea-memorial-post-office


Mr. SUBRAMANYAM. Madam Speaker, I yield myself such time as I may  consume.

I rise today in support of H.R. 6247 and encourage my colleagues to  join me in honoring the life of Mayor Honea by naming the post office  in Marana, Arizona, after him. I yield back the balance of my time.
