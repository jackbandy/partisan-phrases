Officer Zane T. Coolidge Post Office


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701589/officer-zane-t-coolidge-post-office


Mr. SUBRAMANYAM. Madam Speaker, I yield myself such time as I may  consume.

Madam Speaker, I rise today in support of H.R. 3393 as well to  designate the facility of the United States Postal Service at 12208  North 19th Avenue in Phoenix, Arizona, as the Officer Zane T. Coolidge  Post Office.

Zane Coolidge served in the Phoenix Police Department for 5 years. He  was tragically shot while on duty protecting his community.

Madam Speaker, I encourage my colleagues to join me in honoring the  life of Officer Coolidge and support this bill. I yield back the  balance of my time.
