Making Life More Affordable


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700913/making-life-more-affordable


Mr. MENEFEE. Mr. Speaker, when I was 5 years old, Tupac Shakur said:  ``You know, it's funny when it rains it pours. They got money for wars,  but can't feed the poor.''

Those words hit differently today.

Mr. President, nobody wants this war, and the American people cannot  afford it.

I am not an economist, but I do know this: The U.S. war with Iran has  rattled global markets, driven up oil prices more than 40 percent, and  sparked real fears of a worldwide recession. We feel it at home. Gas is  more expensive than it has been in a very long time. Flights cost more,  and groceries cost more.

However, this is bigger than just economics. It is about  accountability. I am a lawyer, and I know that every President swears  an oath to defend the Constitution, and that Constitution empowers  Congress to declare war, not the President. In this instance, Congress  was sidelined. The American people got no clear cause, no strategy, and  no endgame.

I say this as a proud son of two veterans. Their sacrifice meant  something. Mr. Speaker, you do not send our sons and our daughters into  harm's way without purpose, without justification, and without an  imminent threat.

We cannot afford this war, not economically, and not morally. When a  leader promises peace but delivers war, that is not just a policy  failure; that is a betrayal.

Americans do not support this war, and the President should end it  immediately.
