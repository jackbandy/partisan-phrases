Authority for A Committee to Meet


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701953/authority-for-a-committee-to-meet


Mr. MORAN. Mr. President, I have one request for a committee to meet  during today's session of the Senate. It has the approval of the  Majority and Minority Leaders.

Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the  Senate, the following committee was authorized to meet during today's  session of the Senate:                       SUBCOMMITTEE ON STRATEGIC FORCES

The Subcommittee on Strategic Forces of the Committee on Armed  Services is authorized to meet during the session of the Senate on  Monday, April 20, 2026, at 3:30 p.m., to conduct a hearing.
