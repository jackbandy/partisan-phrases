Recognizing Arab American Heritage Month


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701718/recognizing-arab-american-heritage-month


Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, I am deeply proud to serve not only as an  unapologetic Arab-American woman in Congress but to represent one of  the largest Arab-American communities in our country.

That is why I am introducing a resolution recognizing April as Arab  American Heritage Month. This month, we must recognize the rich  history, culture, and achievements of Arab Americans all across our  country.

I know it has been an incredibly difficult time for many of our  families, especially those with loved ones in Lebanon and Palestine.  Even in the midst of pain and uncertainty, let us all continue to show  up for each other, speak out against injustices, and care for each  other with compassion and solidarity.

Mr. Speaker, I am sure you will agree that this is the time and month  to acknowledge Arab-American contributions, inventions, and support  building businesses and showing up for communities and neighbors all  across the country.

To all the neighbors in southeastern Michigan and beyond, happy Arab  American Heritage Month.
