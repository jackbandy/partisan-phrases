Recognizing Carter Pearce


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1702188/recognizing-carter-pearce


Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize  Carter Pearce, a senior at DuBois Area High School, for winning an  international vocabulary competition.

Vocabulary Bowl is sponsored by Vocabulary.com and invites more than  900,000 high school students to compete in a virtual competition, which  lasts over a month.

Carter, who placed first, mastered over 8,000 words. After winning in  the fall, he competed in the spring competition and mastered all the  featured words.

Carter is also a member of the DuBois Area High School Vocabulary  Quiz Bowl team and has helped the team win multiple regional  competitions. Since joining 2 years ago, Carter has helped the team win  four State titles.

Carter graduates later this spring and plans to major in history. His  goal is to become a history teacher.

Mr. Speaker, this is a fantastic achievement, and I thank Carter for  representing Pennsylvania so well. I wish Carter all the best in his  future endeavors.
