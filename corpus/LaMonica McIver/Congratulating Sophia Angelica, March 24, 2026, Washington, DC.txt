Congratulating Sophia Angelica


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701201/congratulating-sophia-angelica


Mrs. McIVER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Sophia Angelica for  her work in empowering children through music, acting, and writing.

Sophia, a singer and songwriter from Argentina, uses her talents to  advocate for human rights across the globe. She works closely with the  United Nations and has traveled internationally to help combat bullying  and discrimination.

Not only does she travel across the United States for her  performances, but she also performed at the sixth Asia-Pacific Human  Rights Summit in Taiwan.

By combining her vocal talents with her passion for activism, Sophia  is making a remarkable impact on children across the world.

On behalf of New Jersey's 10th Congressional District, I congratulate  Sophia for her impactful global advocacy. I hope she keeps doing what  she is doing.
