Recognizing Wonderful College Prep Academy


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701968/recognizing-wonderful-college-prep-academy


Mr. VALADAO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Wonderful College  Prep Academy for its outstanding commitment to leadership, innovation,  and equity in educating the next generation of leaders in Delano and  across the Central Valley.

Founded in 2009 by Lynda and Stewart Resnick, the academy's two  state-of-the-art TK-12 public charter schools have served more than  2,400 students in Lost Hills and Delano. Through a strong focus on  academic excellence, health and wellness, and dedication to its  educators, the academy is preparing students to succeed in higher  education, the workforce, and in life. Notably, a significant portion  of students have graduated with associate degrees and met college  entrance requirements, an extraordinary achievement that reflects the  school's high standards and student-centered approach.

Wonderful College Prep Academy fosters not only academic success but  also a deep sense of community and lifelong skills. Beyond its  traditional pathways, its programs teach students the importance of  agriculture, provide comprehensive health services, and inspire  families to lead healthier lives.

The academy's recognition as the 2026 California Charter Schools  Association Charter School of the Year is a testament to its unwavering  commitment to academic excellence and student success. Additionally,  the Hart Vision Equity and Innovation Award recognizes the academy's  steadfast advocacy to ensure the success of all students, including  those from historically underserved communities.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the House of Representatives to  join me in congratulating Wonderful College Prep Academy on its  outstanding accomplishments and years of service to the community of  Delano and California's Central Valley.
