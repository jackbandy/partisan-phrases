Sharia-Free America


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701405/sharia-free-america


Mr. FULCHER. Mr. Speaker, I am proud to stand beside my colleagues  here today. The rapid growth of the Sharia-Free America Caucus  underscores the importance of this issue to millions of Americans.

Sharia law is an oppressive, radical legal system that enables  violent intolerance of nonbelievers, the subjugation of women, and  stoning or flogging as punishment for theft, adultery, or blasphemy, as  defined through their lens.

Sharia law cannot coexist with the U.S. Constitution and the American  rule of law. Sharia goes against the very principles that we in America  hold dear: the right to practice the religion you hold to be true or  not, the freedom to speak without fear of consequences, and the equal  right of every man or woman to pursue liberty and happiness.

Our Constitution provides guidelines for freedom, for independence,  and prosperity, and the rule of law is written around it. However,  sharia governs every aspect of one's life. That alone makes it  unconstitutional.

As you have heard from my colleagues here, parts of our country are  witnessing just how devastating and conflicting sharia law can be to  our citizens and the American way of life.

We must be clear: Our Constitution, which has made the United States  a beacon of freedom, is and must remain our sole legal foundation, and  that foundation is not negotiable.
