Recognizing Luke Joslyn As Caro Chamber of Commerce's 2025 Junior Citizen of the Year


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701551/recognizing-luke-joslyn-as-caro-chamber-of-commerces-2025-junior-citizen-of-the-year


Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Luke Joslyn,  recipient of the Caro Chamber of Commerce 2025 Junior Citizen of the  Year Award, whose leadership, service, and dedication have made a  meaningful impact on the Caro community.

Luke has demonstrated a strong commitment to service through a wide  range of volunteer efforts in his community. He has supported  initiatives such as the Special Olympics, local food banks, and 4-H  events, while also dedicating his time to youth programs, church  activities, and community improvement projects. Whether assisting at  school events, helping with seasonal projects, or engaging with  community members, he consistently shows a genuine willingness to serve  others.

In addition to his service, Luke has built an impressive record as a  student-athlete. A multi-sport competitor in football, basketball,  track & field, skeet & trap, and wrestling, he has earned numerous  honors at the conference, regional, and state levels. He has also  served as a team captain, demonstrating responsibility and commitment  to his team.

Beyond athletics, Luke has been actively involved in student  leadership and extracurricular activities. Through his roles in student  council, including serving in executive positions, as well as his  participation in youth leadership programs and school organizations, he  has shown a deep commitment to leadership and civic engagement.

Through his participation in school and community activities, Luke  has shown a strong  sense of community spirit and engagement. His character, work ethic,  and willingness to serve others truly set him apart as a role model  among his peers.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Luke  Joslyn for his accomplishments and for the leadership, integrity, and  dedication he has shown as a representative of the Caro community.
