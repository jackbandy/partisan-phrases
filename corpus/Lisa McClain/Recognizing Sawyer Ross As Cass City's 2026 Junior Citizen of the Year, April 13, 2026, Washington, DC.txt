Recognizing Sawyer Ross As Cass City's 2026 Junior Citizen of the Year


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701567/recognizing-sawyer-ross-as-cass-citys-2026-junior-citizen-of-the-year


Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Sawyer Ross,  recipient of the Cass City 2026 Junior Citizen of the Year Award, whose  leadership, dedication, and commitment to excellence have made a  lasting impact on his school and community.

Sawyer has distinguished himself as a well-rounded student and  accomplished athlete, participating in track and field, cross country,  swimming, and soccer. His discipline and perseverance have led to  significant success in track and field, where he has earned numerous  medals. As a team captain for both track and field and cross country,  he leads by example, demonstrating a strong work ethic and a  consistently positive attitude.

In addition to his athletic achievements, Sawyer excels academically  and is a member of the National Honor Society. His academic  accomplishments include earning an economics academic pin, a GPA-based  academic letter, and a Spanish I Certificate of Excellence.

Sawyer's involvement extends beyond the classroom and athletic field.  He is an active member of the Saginaw Striders Track Club, American  Marriage Ministries, and Youth for Christ Bible Study, where he also  serves on the leadership committee team. He has further demonstrated  his commitment to leadership through his work at Bay Shore Camp, where  he served as a group leader and was recently promoted to activities  coordinator, earning recognition for his contributions and being  honored with the Best Cabin Leader Award.

Through his participation in school and community activities, Sawyer  has shown a strong sense of community spirit and engagement. His  character, work ethic, and willingness to serve others truly set him  apart as a role model among his peers.

Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Sawyer  Ross for his outstanding accomplishments and for the leadership he has  demonstrated as a representative of the Cass City community.
