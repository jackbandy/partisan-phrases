Recognizing Deanna Lewis


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700539/recognizing-deanna-lewis


Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, as we begin Women's History Month, I rise  to recognize a true leader from southern Arizona, Deanna Lewis.

In 1984, Deanna became the first woman and the first Black woman to  serve in the Tucson Fire Department. In a profession where few women  had previously worked, she stepped forward anyway, not to make history  but to protect and care for her community.

Over a 20-year career in the fire departments of both Tucson and  Tubac, Deanna demonstrated strength, integrity, and a deep commitment  to southern Arizona families. Because of her, more women now see  themselves in uniform, in leadership, and in public service.

This Women's History Month, we honor Deanna Lewis for her  trailblazing leadership and recommit ourselves to building a future  where every young woman, whether she dreams of fighting fires or  serving in the Halls of Congress, knows there is a place for her.
