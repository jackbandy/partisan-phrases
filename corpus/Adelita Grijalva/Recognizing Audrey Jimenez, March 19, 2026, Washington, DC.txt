Recognizing Audrey Jimenez


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701010/recognizing-audrey-jimenez


Mrs. GRIJALVA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with great pride to  recognize Audrey Jimenez, a remarkable young woman wrestler from  Tucson, Arizona, a graduate from Sunnyside High School in Arizona's  Seventh Congressional District, and a gold medalist at the Pan American  Wrestling Championships.

Audrey represents the very best of our community. She is an  inspirational young woman who has put in early mornings, long  practices, and hard-fought matches to become a champion.

What stands out even more is her heart, her humility, her  determination, and the way she carries herself as a proud Arizonan.

Audrey is a shining example of a role model who opens doors and  inspires other young girls to see themselves in sports where they may  not have seen themselves before.

I have had the privilege of seeing how deeply our community rallies  behind athletes like Audrey, and it is clear she carries that support  with her every time she competes.

We are so proud of Audrey and are excited for her continued success  in the future.
