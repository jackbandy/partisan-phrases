Recognizing We Love Sports Foundation


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1702150/recognizing-we-love-sports-foundation


Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to share the work being done  by Premiere Athletes and the WE Love Sports Foundation led New Rochelle  native, Lawrence Posey.

Lawrence is a former athlete and passionate community leader who  understands the impact when you gather communities around the love of  sports.

I have fond memories from my childhood of playing baseball and  football with the other kids on the block after the streetlights came  on and before my parents called me in the house. These moments taught  me valuable lessons of teamwork, time management, and responsibility.

Today, these kids play games on the same streets and on the fields as  I once did, learning the same valuable lessons.

I commend Lawrence and WE Love Sports and Premiere Athletes for  making it possible for every child to have the opportunity to succeed  and have fun as kids should on and off the field.
