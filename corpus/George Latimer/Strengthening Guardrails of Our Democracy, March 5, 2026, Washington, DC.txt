Strengthening Guardrails of Our Democracy


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700541/strengthening-guardrails-of-our-democracy


Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, the guarantee of free and fair elections is  the very bedrock of our democracy. However, from the moment this term  began, he has made every effort to subvert the electoral process for  political gain. We continue to hear the big lie about the 2020 election  and pressure lawmakers across the country to redraw congressional  districts for political purposes. We saw insurrectionists pardoned.

Most recently, he has called for the party to nationalize voting in  the United States. That is why, this week, I introduced the POINT Act,  because our elections must remain insulated from bad-faith actors who  seek to undermine the will of the voters.

This bill would prohibit the President and senior members of his  administration from knowingly engaging in election interference or  using Federal resources to interfere with an election. It prohibits:  the deployment of our military and Federal officers, if such is likely  to disrupt an election; postal confiscation of all mail ballots; and  ICE or National Guard from looming at the polls.

Mr. Speaker, it is imperative that we strengthen the guardrails of  our democracy against anyone who seeks to abuse power for political  gain.
