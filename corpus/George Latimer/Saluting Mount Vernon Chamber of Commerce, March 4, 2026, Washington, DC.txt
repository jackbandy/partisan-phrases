Saluting Mount Vernon Chamber of Commerce


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1700422/saluting-mount-vernon-chamber-of-commerce


Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the outstanding work of  the men and women who lead the Mount Vernon Chamber of Commerce.

Every month, they hold a Zoom meeting for all of their members and  provide the latest information of value to the businessowners and  retailers who provide goods and services to the people of Mount Vernon.  The guest speakers cover substantive topics such as access to capital,  tax preparation, availability of grant programs, healthcare, personnel  issues, tariffs, and much more.

Every month, I join in virtually to give a Federal issues update. The  same is true for county officials such as County Executive Ken Jenkins  and Shari Rosen Ascher.

The chamber is led by Omar Fields, chairman of the board; Anita Hunt,  board secretary; and former Mount Vernon Councilwoman Lisa Copeland,  who serves as president. They do a tremendous service to the people and  to  the businesses of the city of Mount Vernon.

From the floor of the House of Representatives, we salute the Mount  Vernon Chamber of Commerce.
