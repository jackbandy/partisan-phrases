Providing for Disposition of Senate Amendment to H.R. Department of Homeland Security Appropriations Act, 2026


https://justfacts.votesmart.org/public-statement/1701507/providing-for-disposition-of-senate-amendment-to-hr-department-of-homeland-security-appropriations-act-2026


Mr. STANTON. Mr. Speaker, we should have voted today, we should have  voted yesterday, last week, last month to fund every department in  Homeland Security, but not more money for ICE without changes.

That is what House Democrats are fighting for, and this morning, the  Senate passed a bill unanimously to do just that, every single Senator,  Democrat and Republican. Speaker Johnson called that Senate bill a  joke.

Let me tell you what is not a joke: Cybersecurity experts going  without pay on the same day Iran hacked the Director of the FBI, FEMA  being dismantled in real time, or TSA agents launching GoFundMe  accounts to pay for gas and groceries.

Instead of taking up the Senate bill, House Republicans are choosing  to give even more money to Trump's mass deportation machine beyond the  $75 billion slush fund they gave ICE last summer. There is no  accountability, no oversight, no limits.

Let's be clear about what is happening. DHS is shut down because  Republicans are refusing to listen to the vast majority of the American  people who want to see changes to ICE. I urge my colleagues to vote  ``no.''
